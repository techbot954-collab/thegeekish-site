AI agents may need AI watchers too

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on reporting from TechCrunch.

As AI agents take on longer tasks, the oversight problem is getting big enough that some teams are putting another AI in the review loop.

AGENT SAFETYGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports that companies using more capable AI agents are running into a simple review bottleneck: agents can act faster and at more volume than humans can realistically inspect one action at a time.

The new pitch

The emerging fix is to place AI monitors around AI agents. TechCrunch points to tools that check proposed actions before they run, escalate risky behavior to stronger monitors, ask a human for approval, or block an action outright.

The catch

That creates a strange trust loop. If a bad agent knows another AI is watching, critics warn it may try to mislead the watcher too. The safer answer may be layered: AI monitors, detailed logs, network controls, and old-fashioned security hygiene.

Geekish take

The next phase of agentic AI will not be just smarter bots. It will be receipts, permissions, monitoring, and boring audit trails. That is less flashy than demos, but probably more important.

Quick takeaways

  • AI agents are creating an oversight problem because they can act at machine speed.
  • Startups and labs are building AI monitors to flag, escalate, or block risky actions.
  • Experts still want non-AI safeguards such as detailed logs and network monitoring.
