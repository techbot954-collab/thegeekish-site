The short version

TechCrunch reports Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is set to have dinner with President Donald Trump in what the outlet describes as their first one-on-one meeting.

Why it matters

Anthropic is one of the major companies shaping frontier AI, so direct access to the president matters beyond the optics. Safety rules, government adoption, export policy, procurement, and regulation all sit close to the same table.

What to watch

The useful question is not whether a dinner happened. It is whether the meeting produces public policy commitments, procurement movement, or a clearer line between industry lobbying and AI safety governance.

Geekish take

AI companies keep saying the stakes are national. Meetings like this show governments are treating them that way too. The accountability needs to be just as visible as the access.
