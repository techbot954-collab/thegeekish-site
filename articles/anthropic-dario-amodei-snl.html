The short version

TechCrunch reports Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei received the Saturday Night Live treatment, with the sketch leaning into the uneasy public image around AI builders and their power.

Why it matters

Late-night parody is a cultural signal. It means AI leadership has become recognizable enough for a broad audience to understand the joke, even if the audience does not follow model releases or cloud deals.

Geekish take

AI companies want to be seen as infrastructure, tools, and safety labs. Pop culture is turning them into characters. That shift matters because trust, fear, and ridicule all shape how normal people receive the tech.

Quick takeaways

  • TechCrunch says Anthropic's Dario Amodei was parodied on Saturday Night Live.
  • The moment shows AI leaders are now mainstream enough for broad comedy.
