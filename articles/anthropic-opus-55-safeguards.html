Claude Opus 5.5 arrives with cheaper tokens and stricter guardrails

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on Anthropic's launch post and TechCrunch reporting.

Anthropic is pitching Opus 5.5 as a stronger coding and knowledge-work model that costs less to run while keeping Fable-level restrictions for higher-risk use.

MODEL LAUNCHGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 on September 22. The company says the model leads in agentic coding and knowledge work, and its own page says Opus 5.5 costs 40% less to run than Opus 5 on typical workloads.

What changed

TechCrunch reports output tokens for Opus 5.5 are priced at $20 per million, down from $25 for the previous model, and says other metrics saw similar price drops. Anthropic also says the model is designed to put important information earlier in responses and use less jargon.

The safety angle

The release comes after Anthropic's public push to "pace the frontier." TechCrunch reports Opus 5.5 is subject to the same safeguards as Fable because Anthropic rates it as comparable to Mythos in biology and cybersecurity capabilities.

Geekish take

The interesting part is not just that a bigger model got cheaper. It is that Anthropic is trying to sell capability and caution in the same breath: better coding, lower serving cost, and a more visible safety perimeter.

Quick takeaways

  • Anthropic says Claude Opus 5.5 leads in agentic coding and knowledge work.
  • The company says it costs 40% less to run than Opus 5 on typical workloads.
  • TechCrunch reports Opus 5.5 uses Fable-level safeguards for higher-risk biology and cybersecurity capability areas.
