Apple is softening tracking prompts in parts of Europe

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Apple's developer notice and reporting from TechCrunch.

Apple is not removing App Tracking Transparency in Europe. It is giving developers a less blunt consent screen in selected EU countries after competition pressure.

APP PRIVACYGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Apple says that beginning with iOS 27.2 and iPadOS 27.2, developers in the EU can use an alternative App Tracking Transparency prompt. TechCrunch reports the change follows a multi-year antitrust probe by Germany's federal competition regulator and other European authorities.

What changes

The tracking permission requirement remains. The interface can change: TechCrunch reports that developers can use modified language, formatting, a fuller explanation link, and less alarming choices such as "Allow" and "Reject" instead of the older wording.

Where it applies

Apple says legal requirements mean the alternative prompt is available for apps distributed in Germany, France, Italy, Poland, and Romania. Developers will also be able to re-prompt EU users one year after a previous ATT choice.

Geekish take

This is privacy UX becoming competition policy. The button labels matter because consent screens are not neutral; the way a choice looks can reshape ad businesses, app revenue, and what users think they are agreeing to.

Quick takeaways

  • Apple's ATT rules stay in place, but parts of the EU get a new prompt option.
  • The change follows pressure from European competition authorities.
  • Developers get more room to explain why they want tracking permission.
