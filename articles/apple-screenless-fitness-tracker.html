Apple is reportedly testing a screenless fitness tracker

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on The Verge's report citing Bloomberg's Apple wearable reporting.

A new report says Apple is exploring a Whoop-style device built around continuous health tracking instead of another tiny screen.

WEARABLESGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports, citing Bloomberg, that Apple is working on a new fitness tracker with a screenless design similar to Whoop. The reported prototype uses a thin fabric band with a sensor-equipped computing module.

Do not expect it soon

The report says the device is still early and likely would not arrive before 2028 if Apple decides to ship it. That makes this a direction-of-travel story, not a launch story.

Geekish take

Apple already owns the mainstream smartwatch lane. A screenless tracker would be a different bet: less glanceable computer, more always-on health layer. That could appeal to people who want Apple health tracking without wearing another display.

Quick takeaways

  • The Verge says Apple is exploring a Whoop-like screenless fitness tracker.
  • Bloomberg's report describes a fabric band and sensor-equipped module.
  • The device is reportedly early and may not arrive before 2028.
