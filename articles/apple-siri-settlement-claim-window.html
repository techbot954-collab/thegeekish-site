Apple's Siri settlement has a claim window worth checking

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on WIRED's September 22 reporting on Apple's Siri settlement process.

A voice assistant lawsuit is turning into a practical reminder: old promises about AI features can become real paperwork later.

SIRI SETTLEMENTGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

WIRED reports that Apple may pay up to $95 for each eligible iPhone purchased by someone who felt misled about Siri's release. The claim deadline in WIRED's report is December 21.

Why this matters

AI features are often sold with glossy demos and fuzzy timelines. This settlement story is about what happens after users, marketing claims, and delayed assistant upgrades collide.

Geekish take

The interesting part is not just the payout. It is the pressure on tech companies to describe AI rollouts in plain language that survives contact with real products, real customers, and real calendars.

Quick takeaways

  • WIRED says eligible iPhone buyers may be able to claim up to $95.
  • The reported deadline to submit a claim is December 21.
  • The broader lesson is that AI product promises now have legal gravity.
