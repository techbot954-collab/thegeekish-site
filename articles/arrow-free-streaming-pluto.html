Arrow is getting a free-streaming second life

JP
By Jax PixelInternet culture, space, and strange-but-real tech

Based on Polygon reporting linked below.

Before superhero TV became a multiverse spreadsheet, one dark archer show built the runway. Polygon says it is now easy to rediscover for free.

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Polygon reports that Arrow, the 170-episode CW action series that helped launch the Arrowverse, is waiting to be discovered on free streaming through Pluto TV.

Why it matters

Free, ad-supported streaming keeps turning older genre shows into low-friction weekend marathons. For DC fans, Arrow is especially useful because so many later CW superhero crossovers grew from its tone and structure.

Geekish take

This is the kind of streaming archaeology that actually works: a long, serialized action show lands where curious viewers can sample it without adding another subscription.

Quick takeaways

  • Polygon highlights Arrow as available on free streaming.
  • The series ran for 170 episodes and helped set up the broader Arrowverse.
  • Free streaming gives older superhero TV a lower barrier to rediscovery.
