Automattic has a new board after its CEO fight

JP
By Jax PixelApps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on TechCrunch reporting linked below.

The company behind WordPress.com, Tumblr, and WooCommerce is still sorting through a very public governance drama.

OPEN WEBGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports Automattic CEO Matt Mullenweg has rebuilt the company's board after previous directors tried to put him on leave. Automattic owns WordPress.com, Tumblr, WooCommerce, and other web products.

What TechCrunch found

According to the report, the new board includes author Hugh Howey, author Amy Chan, and IRL co-founders Henry Khachatryan and Krutal Desai. TechCrunch says new advisers have also joined, including former Whoop CTO Jaime Waydo, June co-founder Matt Van Horn, and KISSmetrics co-founder Hiten Shah.

The backstory

TechCrunch reports the prior board voted earlier in September to put Mullenweg on paid leave, but he retook control about 33 hours later and removed or accepted the resignations of directors involved. Mullenweg confirmed the board additions after publication.

Geekish take

This matters because Automattic is not just another startup with internal drama. Its products sit under a giant slice of the web, so governance fights there can ripple into creators, publishers, plugin makers, and open-web developers.

Quick takeaways

  • TechCrunch reports Automattic has a new board after a failed attempt to put CEO Matt Mullenweg on leave.
  • The company owns WordPress.com, Tumblr, WooCommerce, and other web products.
  • Mullenweg confirmed the board additions and said the next six months may shape Automattic's next 20 years.
