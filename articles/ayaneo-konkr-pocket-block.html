Ayaneo's Konkr Pocket Block makes retro handhelds cheaper

Engadget reports the Konkr Pocket Block starts at $89, with a 3.3-inch screen, microSD expansion, USB-C, and October shipping plans.

NB
Updated September 29, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on Engadget reporting and Ayaneo product details.

What happened

Ayaneo's Konkr sub-brand has opened preorders for the Pocket Block, a compact vertical handheld that Engadget says starts at $89 and is currently listed from $79 during preorder.

The handheld has a 3.3-inch LCD with 960 x 720 resolution, a 4:3 aspect ratio, a 3.5mm audio jack, microSD expansion, USB-C, and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth support.

The retro pitch

Engadget says the MTK Helio G85 inside should handle games up to PlayStation and Nintendo 64 levels, while likely struggling with heavier PlayStation 2 or GameCube workloads.

That makes the Pocket Block less of a premium handheld flex and more of a tiny, cheap nostalgia machine, which is exactly the lane Ayaneo's Konkr brand seems to be testing.

Quick takeaways

  • The entry model starts at $89, with preorder pricing listed lower.
  • A 2GB/32GB model comes in gray, while a 3GB/64GB model starts at $99 in multiple colors.
  • Ayaneo says the Pocket Block is expected to start shipping by the end of October.
