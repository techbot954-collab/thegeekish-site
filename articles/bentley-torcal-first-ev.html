Bentley's first EV is the Torcal, and it still wants drama

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on Ars Technica reporting and Bentley source material cited by Ars.

Bentley's battery-electric debut is not trying to be quiet transportation. It is trying to make luxury EVs feel theatrical.

LUXURY EVSGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Ars Technica reports Bentley has formally unveiled the Torcal, the brand's first fully electric vehicle. Ars says the Torcal will go on sale early next year as a 2028 model.

The numbers

According to Ars, the standard Torcal produces 810 hp and 880 lb-ft of torque, good for a 3.3-second 0-60 mph run. The Torcal S raises output to 875 hp and 995 lb-ft, with a claimed 2.8-second 0-60 mph time and a 162 mph top speed.

The platform story

Ars notes Bentley shares technology with Porsche inside the Volkswagen Group. That makes the Torcal part luxury product, part group-platform showcase, and part answer to the question of how legacy prestige brands move into EVs without losing identity.

Geekish take

The important detail is not just that Bentley made an EV. It is that Bentley is still selling sound, feel, and occasion. The luxury EV race is becoming less about "electric" and more about personality.

Quick takeaways

  • The Torcal is Bentley's first battery-electric vehicle.
  • Ars reports the Torcal S can hit 60 mph in 2.8 seconds.
  • The EV is expected to go on sale early next year as a 2028 model.
