BepiColombo is finally closing in on Mercury

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on WIRED's September 21 report and ESA's BepiColombo mission page.

After a long, tricky route through the inner solar system, the Mercury mission is moving from patience to payoff.

MERCURY MISSIONGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

WIRED reports that BepiColombo is approaching Mercury after eight years in space. The mission is expected to help scientists study Mercury's magnetic field and internal structure.

The mission stack

ESA describes BepiColombo as a joint ESA-JAXA mission to Mercury. That matters because Mercury is hard to reach and hard to study: the spacecraft has to manage the Sun's heat, orbital mechanics, and a planet with a surprisingly interesting magnetic environment.

Geekish take

This is the slow-burn side of space tech. No instant spectacle, just years of navigation so a spacecraft can arrive where the science gets good.
