Bernie Sanders wants to ban artificial superintelligence

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on Sen. Bernie Sanders' September 23 press release, the bill PDF, and The Verge's coverage.

The loudest AI-policy proposal of the day is not a tweak around disclosure. It is a proposed federal stop sign for superintelligence.

AI POLICYGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar introduced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act on September 23. Sanders' office says the bill would create a new federal agency to ban artificial superintelligence and pause advanced AI development until federal testing and oversight rules are in place.

Why it matters

The Verge reports the proposal defines the target as systems capable of the destruction or disempowerment of humanity, including by overthrowing the government. The bill also puts possible prison penalties on the table for violations, which makes it a much sharper intervention than the usual audit-and-report approach.

The Geekish angle

Even if the bill does not become law as written, it moves the AI debate into a more concrete place: who gets to decide when a system is too capable to build, and what happens if a lab ignores that line?

Geekish take

The story is not just "AI scary." It is a sign that superintelligence is becoming a legislative category, not just a conference-panel phrase.

Quick takeaways

  • Sanders and Casar introduced the bill on September 23, 2026.
  • The proposal would create a federal agency and pause advanced AI development pending oversight.
  • The Verge reports the bill also includes penalties for building banned superintelligence systems.
