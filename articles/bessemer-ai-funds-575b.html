Bessemer has $5.75B more for the AI startup stack

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on TechCrunch reporting and Bessemer's funding announcement linked below.

The AI boom is still pulling giant checks into venture capital, and Bessemer is spelling out where it wants to place them: compute, infrastructure, models, developer tools, apps, and agents.

AI VENTUREGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports that Bessemer Venture Partners raised $5.75 billion across two new funds to invest across the AI stack.

Where the money goes

According to TechCrunch, Bessemer set aside $1.75 billion for seed and early-stage investing and $4 billion for growth-stage startups. The firm says it has invested in more than 260 AI-native companies since 2022.

The startup map

The target is broad: compute, infrastructure, foundation models, developer tools, app-layer startups, and agentic technology. That says investors are still treating AI less like one product category and more like a full rebuild of the software stack.

Geekish take

The number is loud, but the split is the signal. Early AI companies still need risk capital, while the growth fund says the winners are expected to stay private longer and eat more money before they reach the public markets.

Quick takeaways

  • Bessemer raised $5.75 billion across two new funds.
  • TechCrunch says $1.75 billion is for seed and early-stage deals, with $4 billion for growth startups.
  • The firm is aiming across compute, infrastructure, models, dev tools, apps, and agents.
