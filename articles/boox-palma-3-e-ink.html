Boox's Palma 3 keeps the phone-shaped e-reader idea alive

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on WIRED's report on Boox's latest E Ink launches.

Boox announced three new E Ink devices, but the Palma 3 is the weird little one to watch: an Android 16, phone-shaped e-reader built around low-stimulation reading instead of full smartphone chaos.

E INK GEARGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

WIRED reports that Boox announced three E Ink products: the $580 Note Air6 C, the $580 Note Mini C, and the $340 Palma 3.

The Palma 3 pitch

The Palma 3 is a palm-sized E Ink device with Android 16, a 6.13-inch black-and-white screen, a dual-tone front light, an ambient light sensor, rear camera, dual microphones, and a customizable smart button. WIRED says Boox promotes it as a low-stimulation second screen.

The notebook side

The Note Air6 C and Note Mini C use Kaleido 3 color displays. The Air6 C has a 10.3-inch screen and is available now, while the 8.52-inch Mini C is listed as coming soon. WIRED reports both include octa-core processors, 6 GB of RAM, 64 GB of storage, and microSD expansion up to 2 TB.

Geekish take

The Palma 3 is interesting because it refuses to be a normal phone. A small Android E Ink slab is a niche gadget, sure, but it also hits a real mood: people want pocketable screens that read well without dragging the whole attention machine along.

Quick takeaways

  • Boox announced the Palma 3, Note Air6 C, and Note Mini C.
  • The Palma 3 is a $340, phone-shaped E Ink reader running Android 16.
  • The Note Air6 C is available now; the Palma 3 and Note Mini C are coming soon.
