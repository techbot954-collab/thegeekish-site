Bose finally starts tuning into Auracast

A new firmware update is bringing Bluetooth LE Audio and Auracast beta support to Bose's flagship over-ear headphones.

JP
Updated September 28, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on The Verge's report and Bose support materials.

What happened

The Verge reports that Bose's QuietComfort Ultra Headphones Gen 2 are getting beta support for Bluetooth LE Audio and Auracast through firmware update 10.12.12. The update also improves audio over wired USB connections, including game/chat balance and better compatibility with web conferencing apps.

The headline feature is Auracast, the Bluetooth broadcast tech designed to let one source send audio to many compatible headphones or speakers at once. Think airport gate announcements, lecture halls, venue audio, shared TV listening, or accessibility setups where public audio can be joined without pairing drama.

Why it matters

Auracast was announced in 2022, but mainstream adoption has been slow and uneven. Bose entering the lane gives the format a recognizable headphone brand at exactly the moment public-audio and assistive-listening use cases need more consumer hardware support.

This is still a beta rollout, so the practical story is not "everyone has it now." It is more interesting than that: another premium audio maker is treating broadcast Bluetooth as a feature worth shipping, not just a standards-body demo.

Quick takeaways

  • The first Bose model called out is the QuietComfort Ultra Headphones Gen 2.
  • The firmware is labeled 10.12.12 and adds Bluetooth LE Audio plus Auracast beta support.
  • The same update improves wired USB audio behavior for gaming and conferencing.
