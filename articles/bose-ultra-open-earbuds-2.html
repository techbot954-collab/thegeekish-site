Bose is making open earbuds louder, bassier, and sportier

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on The Verge reporting and Bose product pages linked below.

Open earbuds are still trying to solve the same trick: give you music without sealing you off from the world. Bose is betting better bass and battery life will make the format feel less compromised.

OPEN AUDIOGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports that Bose announced Ultra Open Earbuds (2nd Gen) and a cheaper Sport Open Earbuds model, both available for preorder on September 19 with availability starting October 1.

What changed

According to The Verge, the second-gen Ultra Open Earbuds add higher volume, deeper bass, a Cinema Mode using Bose TrueSpatial tech, up to nine hours of earbud battery life, and a redesigned wireless-charging case.

The sportier version

The new Sport Open Earbuds use a thinner cuff with a soft silicone matte finish for a more secure fit, with Bose pitching them for movement-heavy use like running and workouts.

Geekish take

Open earbuds live or die on whether they stop feeling like a compromise. More bass, better battery, and a workout-focused model are exactly the areas Bose needed to clean up.

Quick takeaways

  • Ultra Open Earbuds (2nd Gen) are listed at $299, according to The Verge.
  • Sport Open Earbuds are listed at $199.
  • Both are expected to be available October 1 after September 19 preorders.
