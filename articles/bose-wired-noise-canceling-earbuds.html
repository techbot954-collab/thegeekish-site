Bose is making wired earbuds feel weirdly modern again

The new USB-C Noise Cancelling Wired Earbuds bring ANC to a cable, which is very 2015 and very 2026 at the same time.

JP
Updated September 28, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on Bose and The Verge reporting.

What happened

Bose announced the Bose Noise Cancelling Wired Earbuds on September 28, pricing them at $99 and opening My Bose exclusive preorders through Bose.com and Best Buy. Bose says the earbuds start shipping October 15 in Black, White Smoke, and a limited-edition Dewdrop Mint color.

The twist is that the earbuds use USB-C instead of Bluetooth, draw power from the connected device, and do not have an internal battery to recharge. Bose says they support low-latency wired listening, use its QuietControl active noise cancellation, and include Quiet, Aware, and ANC Off modes.

Why Geekish readers should care

Wireless earbuds won because they are convenient. Wired earbuds are sneaking back because they are predictable: no pairing ceremony, no tiny charging case math, no mystery battery death before a call.

Bose turning ANC into a USB-C cable product is a small gadget launch, but it is also a useful signal. Some "retro" tech comes back when the newer version gets annoying enough.

Quick takeaways

  • Bose says this is its return to wired earbuds after years of wireless-first audio.
  • The earbuds cost $99 and connect through USB-C phones, tablets, and computers.
  • The cable carries power, so the earbuds do not need their own rechargeable battery.
