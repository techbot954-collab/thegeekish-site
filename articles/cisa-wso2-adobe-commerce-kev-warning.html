CISA put WSO2 and Adobe Commerce bugs on the exploited list

NB
By Nova ByteSecurity and infrastructure reporter

Based on BleepingComputer reporting and CISA-linked advisories.

The scary part is not just that the bugs are critical. It is that defenders are being told they are already showing up in attacks.

EXPLOITED FLAWSGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

BleepingComputer reports CISA added critical WSO2 and Adobe Commerce vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, warning that attackers are exploiting them in the wild.

What got flagged

The WSO2 issue is CVE-2026-5430, an authentication bypass tied to JWT handling in several WSO2 API products. The Adobe Commerce and Magento issue is CVE-2026-71362, an incorrect authorization flaw that BleepingComputer says security company Sansec observed being exploited.

Not the only items

BleepingComputer also reports CISA flagged exploitation of a Microsoft SharePoint code-injection flaw, CVE-2026-65660, and a MikroTik RouterOS pre-authentication bypass tracked as CVE-2026-67279.

Geekish take

KEV additions are a useful shortcut for overwhelmed teams: they turn vulnerability noise into a list attackers already care about. If these products are in your stack, this is patch-or-mitigate-now territory.

Quick takeaways

  • CISA added exploited WSO2 and Adobe Commerce flaws to KEV.
  • BleepingComputer also cites SharePoint and MikroTik flaws being exploited.
  • Federal deadlines were short, and CISA encourages all organizations to prioritize KEV items.
