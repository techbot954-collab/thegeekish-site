Citrix confirmed two NetScaler zero-days are being exploited

Based on BleepingComputer's report on exploited Citrix NetScaler flaws.

The short version

BleepingComputer reports Citrix confirmed that two critical NetScaler remote code execution vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772, are being exploited in attacks. Citrix has released security updates for the flaws, according to the report.

Why this matters

NetScaler sits close to sensitive traffic, authentication, and application access. When an edge product has exploited RCE flaws, the risk is not theoretical for teams that have internet-facing deployments.

Geekish take

Security teams do not need a cinematic breach narrative here. They need inventory, patch status, exposure checks, and logs reviewed for signs that attackers got there first.
