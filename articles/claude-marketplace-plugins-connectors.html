Claude Marketplace turns AI tools into a storefront fight

AI assistants are no longer just chat boxes. Anthropic is trying to make Claude a place where teams discover connectors, plugins, agents, and partner products.

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

BleepingComputer reports that Anthropic launched Claude Marketplace with more than 2,000 connectors and plugins. The marketplace also includes Claude-powered products from partners and consulting or systems integration help from firms including Accenture, Boston Consulting Group, and Deloitte.

Why this matters

The move points to a larger fight over where AI work actually happens. If users can find trusted integrations, agents, and workplace tools inside Claude, the assistant becomes less like a single app and more like an operating layer for work software.

Geekish take

The hard part is not launching an AI storefront. The hard part is making it useful after the first wave of listings arrives. Anthropic needs quality control, clear trust signals, and enough developer energy to avoid becoming another searchable drawer of half-used tools.

Quick takeaways

  • Anthropic's Claude Marketplace is public, according to BleepingComputer.
  • The launch includes more than 2,000 connectors and plugins.
  • Developers can build connectors and plugins using MCP and Agent Skills.
