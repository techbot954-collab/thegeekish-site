The short version

Polygon reports former Dungeons & Dragons leads Chris Perkins and Jeremy Crawford say Critical Role's Daggerheart represents a "fundamental shift" for tabletop role-playing games.

Why it matters

Critical Role is not just a show anymore. Its publishing moves can change what fans expect from systems, creators, streaming communities, and the business around tabletop play.

Geekish take

The interesting part is not whether one fantasy RPG replaces another. It is that actual-play culture, designer migration, and community-first publishing are starting to reshape the table itself.

Quick takeaways

  • Polygon interviewed former D&D leads Chris Perkins and Jeremy Crawford.
  • They discussed moving into Critical Role's Daggerheart ecosystem.
  • The story frames Daggerheart as part of a larger TTRPG design shift.
