Disney+ and Hulu prices are climbing again

JP
By Jax PixelApps, social tech, and internet culture

Based on Engadget's September 23 report, Disney+ price help pages, and Bloomberg reporting linked by Engadget.

Streamflation is still undefeated. The latest Disney+ and Hulu changes make the bundle math more important than ever.

STREAMINGGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports Disney is raising prices on many of its streaming plans. The ad-free Disney+ plan is moving to $21.50 per month, a 13 percent increase, while the ad-free Disney+ and Hulu bundle is rising by $2 to $22 per month.

What changes

Ad-supported Disney+ and Hulu plans are also going up, with Engadget reporting Disney+ with ads and Hulu with ads will each be $12.50 per month. The ad-supported bundle that includes both services is staying at $13 per month.

Why it matters

For casual viewers, the practical takeaway is that standalone ad-free streaming keeps getting harder to justify. Engadget notes this is the fourth Disney+ price increase across four years, which turns annual price reviews into part of the subscription routine.

Geekish take

The interesting part is not just that the bill is higher. It is that Disney is making the cheap bundle feel like the only sane default, which nudges viewers toward ads even if they started streaming to avoid the old TV experience.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says Disney+ Premium is increasing to $21.50 per month.
  • The ad-free Disney+ and Hulu bundle is rising to $22 per month.
  • The ad-supported Disney+ and Hulu bundle remains $13 per month.
