Dyson's $500 CameraJet toothbrush has a component mystery

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on WIRED reporting linked below.

A camera-guided toothbrush already sounded like maximum gadget energy. The stranger part is now the silence around what went wrong.

GADGET RISKGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

WIRED reports that Dyson's CameraJet, a $499 electric toothbrush with a camera-guided rinsing system, has become hard to buy after early complaints and reports of failures.

What Dyson says

According to WIRED, Dyson said the CameraJet has not been recalled and is seeing strong demand, while also saying it identified a single component issue affecting a small number of early batches.

What is unclear

WIRED says Dyson would not explain the specific component, the problem it caused, how many units were affected globally, or why the company had not issued a formal recall for faulty early batches.

Geekish take

The CameraJet is a perfect reminder that adding sensors to a simple product raises the bar for trust. If a gadget is meant to live next to water, the mystery should not be whether water can get inside it.

Quick takeaways

  • WIRED describes the CameraJet as a $499 camera-guided toothbrush.
  • Dyson says a small number of early batches had a component issue.
  • Repair experts quoted by WIRED discussed possible water-ingress explanations, but Dyson has not confirmed the specific fault.
