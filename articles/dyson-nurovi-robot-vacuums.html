Dyson's new Nurovi robot vacuums are chasing smarter mess detection

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on WIRED's hands-on product reporting and Dyson product details cited there.

Dyson's robot vacuum story now has three Nurovi models, lidar mapping, and a pricier version that uses UV light to help find stains. The pitch is simple: less wandering, more targeted cleaning.

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

WIRED reports that Dyson has launched three robot vacuums in its new Nurovi line: the R1 Nurovi Dry, the R2 Nurovi Wash+Dry, and the R3 Nurovi Spot+Scrub UV.

What changed

All three models use lidar navigation, a shift from Dyson's previous camera-based robot vacuum approach. WIRED says the line also uses Dyson's Twin-Capture System for dust and debris on hard floors and carpets.

The lineup

The R1 is vacuum-only at $550. The R2 is a $700 vacuum-mop hybrid and is listed as a Costco exclusive. The R3 is the $1,200 top model, with higher suction and UV-assisted stain detection.

Geekish take

The robot vacuum category is crowded, so Dyson needs more than the logo. Lidar is table stakes now, but if the UV stain feature actually helps the robot clean what humans notice, that is the fun part to watch.

Quick takeaways

  • Dyson's Nurovi line includes three robot vacuum models.
  • All three use lidar navigation instead of Dyson's older camera-first setup.
  • The high-end R3 adds UV-assisted stain detection and stronger suction.
