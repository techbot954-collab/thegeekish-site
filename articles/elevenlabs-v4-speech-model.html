ElevenLabs v4 pushes voice AI toward expressive control

The new model is not just about cleaner speech. It is about steering tone, language, latency, and cloned voices with less friction.

MC
Updated September 28, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on TechCrunch reporting.

What happened

TechCrunch reports that ElevenLabs launched two new speech models, ElevenLabs v4 and v4 Turbo, with more expression control, lower latency for voice agents, and support for more than 90 languages.

The report says the v4 architecture allows faster cloning and can clone a voice from 10 seconds of audio. It also says v4 expands ElevenLabs' inline expression tags, lets users stack multiple tags, and follows the sequence while reading longer chunks of text.

Why Geekish readers should care

The interesting part is not that synthetic voices sound better. That has been the obvious direction for a while. The more useful shift is control: voice agents that can respond faster, handle escalations differently, and move between languages without sounding bolted together.

That also raises the stakes for disclosure and misuse. The easier voice cloning gets, the more important it becomes for products to pair expressive generation with strong consent, provenance, and fraud defenses.

Quick takeaways

  • TechCrunch says v4 and v4 Turbo target expression control and lower-latency voice agents.
  • The new model supports more than 90 languages, up from 70 in the previous version.
  • TechCrunch reports v4 can clone a voice with 10 seconds of audio.
