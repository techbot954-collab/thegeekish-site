AI's next data-center bottleneck is the power grid

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on TechCrunch reporting and public company context linked below.

The AI buildout is not just a chip story anymore. A new coalition wants to locate enough electricity access for the next wave of data centers.

GRID HUNTGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports that Google, Nvidia, Anthropic, and Emerald AI are part of a new coalition looking for 100 GW of grid capacity for future data centers.

Why 100 GW matters

AI companies can buy chips and lease buildings faster than utilities can add transmission, generation, and interconnection capacity. A target measured in gigawatts shows the industry is now treating electricity access as a strategic supply chain.

The Geekish angle

The practical question is no longer only who has the best model. It is who can run enough compute, in enough places, without making the grid the choke point.

Quick takeaways

  • TechCrunch says the coalition includes Google, Nvidia, Anthropic, and Emerald AI.
  • The goal is to identify 100 GW of grid capacity for new data centers.
  • Power access is becoming part of the AI infrastructure race.
