Foldables are getting close to tablet duty, but not for everyone

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets and practical tech reporter

Based on Engadget reporting from September 27, 2026.

A foldable can shrink the two-device problem. It does not magically erase tablet comfort, price, battery, or app-layout tradeoffs.

FOLDABLESGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget asks whether a foldable phone can replace a tablet and notes that modern foldables can open to nearly the size of an iPad mini.

Why it matters

The pitch is obvious: one pocketable device for phone tasks and small-tablet tasks. The catch is that tablet replacement depends on reading comfort, app support, multitasking, durability, and whether the price makes sense.

The Geekish read

For light reading, messaging, maps, and travel, a foldable can be the neat answer. For drawing, long video sessions, keyboard work, or anything that wants a bigger stable canvas, a separate tablet still has a job.

Quick takeaways

  • Foldables reduce the need to carry both a phone and a small tablet.
  • Tablet replacement depends on apps, comfort, and durability.
  • The price gap still makes the decision feel personal, not automatic.
