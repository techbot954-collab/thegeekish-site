You may not need paid distraction-blocking software

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets and practical tech reporter

Based on WIRED reporting from September 27, 2026.

The best focus app might be the boring one already sitting in your phone settings, if it creates enough friction at the right moment.

FOCUS TOOLSGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

WIRED says people do not necessarily need to pay for distraction-blocking software, because free iOS and Android tools can add enough friction to help them put the phone down.

Why it matters

The goal is not a perfect productivity system. It is creating a small pause between impulse and app, whether that means focus modes, app limits, notification controls, or lock-screen friction.

The Geekish read

Paid focus apps can be useful, but the first experiment should be free: make the phone slightly less fun to grab. If that works, you have solved the problem without adding another subscription to manage.

Quick takeaways

  • WIRED highlights free tools on iOS and Android for reducing phone distractions.
  • The winning tactic is friction, not punishment.
  • Try built-in focus controls before paying for another blocker.
