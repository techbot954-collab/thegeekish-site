The short version

Google says Gemini is rolling out more connected apps so the assistant can help users tackle tasks across external services. The named additions include Adobe, Airtable, Linear, Peloton, and more.

Why it matters

AI assistants become much more useful when they can reach the tools where work actually happens. Connected apps are the bridge between a helpful answer and a completed task, but they also raise the stakes for permissions, accuracy, and user control.

The workflow angle

Apps like Airtable and Linear point toward practical work coordination, while Adobe and Peloton hint at creative and lifestyle use cases. Google is trying to make Gemini feel less like a single app and more like a command layer over many apps.

Geekish take

This is where the assistant race gets real. The flashiest demos are fun, but the winner may be the AI that can reliably move between your tools without making permission management feel like a second job.

Quick takeaways

  • Google says Gemini is adding more connected apps.
  • The list includes Adobe, Airtable, Linear, Peloton, and more.
  • The update pushes Gemini closer to an app-to-app task assistant.
