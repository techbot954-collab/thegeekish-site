Gemini can call local businesses for Pixel 11 users

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on The Verge and Engadget reporting linked below.

Google is testing a very practical AI-agent pitch: let the chatbot wait on hold so you do not have to.

PHONE AGENTSGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports Google is launching an early experiment on Pixel 11 that lets users ask Gemini to call local businesses for tasks like making reservations, checking stock, or rescheduling appointments. Engadget reports the preliminary rollout is for select Gemini subscribers with a Pixel 11.

How it works

Instead of starting the call yourself, you ask Gemini in the app. The assistant introduces itself, handles phone menus, waits on hold, and shows a live transcript while the user keeps control.

Why it matters

This is the kind of AI feature that could feel useful immediately because it tackles a specific annoyance. It also tests whether businesses and customers are comfortable with more bot-to-human phone calls.

Geekish take

The success case is simple: fewer wasted minutes on hold. The failure case is also simple: awkward calls, confused staff, and another layer between people and small businesses. The live transcript and user control will matter.

Quick takeaways

  • Gemini can call businesses for certain Pixel 11 users in an early experiment.
  • The feature can handle reservations, stock checks, and appointment changes.
  • Users remain in control through a live transcript view.
