Google is pushing agentic shopping deeper into ads and checkout

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, commerce, and creator gear reporter

Based on Google's September 16 agentic commerce update.

Google's newest retail push is less about a normal shopping search and more about AI that answers product questions, measures discovery, and helps move shoppers toward checkout.

AI COMMERCEGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Google says it is rolling out a set of agentic commerce updates for retailers, including broader AI performance insights in Merchant Center, a U.S. beta for Business Agent inside YouTube ads, and new Universal Commerce Protocol checkout capabilities.

AI shopping is becoming measurable

AI performance insights are now generally available to businesses in Australia, Canada, India, New Zealand, and the U.S. Google says the feature shows how brands and products are being discovered across surfaces like AI Mode and AI Overviews, including share-of-voice comparisons.

The YouTube ad angle

Google is inviting U.S. retailers into a Business Agent for YouTube ads beta. The pitch is that viewers can ask complex product questions and get tailored guidance without leaving the YouTube ad context.

Geekish take

This is a preview of shopping where the assistant becomes part of the ad, the search result, and the checkout path. For buyers, that could mean less tab-hopping. For sellers, it means product feeds, loyalty data, videos, and conversational attributes become the new shelf space.

Quick takeaways

  • Google is expanding AI performance insights for merchants in five countries.
  • Business Agent for YouTube ads is entering a U.S. retailer beta.
  • Universal Commerce Protocol updates add cart transfer and checkout-flow testing.
