AI Ads - September 23, 2026

Google gives AI Max advertisers clearer reporting

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Google's Ads and Commerce update.

The pitch is simple: if AI is going to help run search campaigns, advertisers want to see what it actually did.

AI ADSGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Google says AI Max is getting a new reporting feature in Google Ads that shows a unified view of the Search ads journey: the search terms that triggered ads, which creative assets appeared, and where users landed on the advertiser's site.

The language expansion

Google is also bringing the closed beta of AI Brief to Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, and Spanish. AI Brief lets advertisers guide AI Max with plain-language context about their business, audience, and messaging.

What is still unknown

Google says more details and availability are coming later this year, so this is not a full public rollout yet. The useful part to watch is whether the new reporting gives marketers enough visibility to trust AI-generated ad decisions.

Geekish take

AI ad tools are moving from "let the model optimize it" to "show me the chain of choices." That is the right direction. Automation is easier to accept when the dashboard can explain the path from search term to creative to landing page.

Quick takeaways

  • Google is adding a unified AI Max reporting view inside Google Ads.
  • AI Brief beta support is expanding to seven more languages.
  • Google says more availability details are coming later this year.
