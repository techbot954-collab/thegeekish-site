Android Enterprise is getting more AI controls for work phones

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on Google's Android Enterprise update for 2026.

Google is pitching Android Enterprise as a safer way to bring AI and mixed-reality devices into company fleets without making IT teams lose the plot.

ANDROID ENTERPRISEGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Google says Android Enterprise is evolving for the modern workforce with updates meant to help IT teams safeguard corporate data while giving workers room to use newer tools. The source page calls out Gemini AI controls and XREAL Aura management as part of the 2026 update.

Why it matters

Work phones are no longer just email rectangles. They are becoming AI endpoints, identity devices, camera systems, and sometimes gateways into headsets or glasses. That makes management controls more important, especially when companies want AI features without handing every app the keys.

Geekish take

This is the unglamorous side of the AI rollout, but it is the side that decides what actually gets deployed. If Google wants Gemini in more workplaces, the boring admin console has to become part of the product story.

Quick takeaways

  • Google published its 2026 Android Enterprise update on September 23.
  • The announcement highlights Gemini AI controls and XREAL Aura management.
  • The larger theme is safer AI and device management for workplace fleets.
