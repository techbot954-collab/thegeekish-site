Google Beam is getting a real-world expansion test

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Google's Beam expansion announcement.

Google's 3D-ish video meeting system is moving beyond demos with a six-country rollout, HP hardware, and rooms people can book through Industrious.

WORK TECHGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Google says Beam units are now shipping to customers in the U.S., Canada, the U.K., France, Germany, and Japan. The system is delivered as HP Dimension with Google Beam and works with Google Meet and Zoom.

The business pitch

Google says it has 18 channel partners ready to deploy and support Beam. In an eight-week internal study, Google says teams using Beam felt 50% more connected, found feedback 33% easier to understand, and saw a 21% drop in follow-up meetings.

The coworking angle

Google is also partnering with Industrious on the first Beam extended network. Starting in October, Google says people can book HP Dimension with Google Beam at select Industrious locations in Atlanta, Chicago, New York City, and Palo Alto.

Geekish take

The interesting part is the access model. If Beam stays locked inside corporate campuses, it is a fancy room. If coworking locations make it bookable, Google gets a much better test of whether immersive meetings are a tool or just a very expensive demo.

Quick takeaways

  • Google Beam is expanding to customers in six countries.
  • HP Dimension with Google Beam supports Google Meet and Zoom.
  • Industrious locations will offer bookable Beam rooms in four U.S. cities starting in October.
