DevFest 2026 is turning agentic AI into a workshop circuit

Google's community developer season starts October 1, and this year's pitch is less keynote spectacle, more hands-on practice with AI tools.

NB
Updated September 28, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on Google's DevFest announcement.

What happened

Google says DevFest 2026 will run from October 1 through December 31, with more than 800 community-led events planned across 115 countries. The theme is "Build, Secure, Scale: Developers and Builders in the Agentic Era."

The company says local Google Developer Groups will shape the events around hands-on codelabs, workshops, and "agent-athons" using tools such as Gemini, Google AI Studio, Google Cloud, Firebase, Android, Flutter, Angular, and Web MCP.

Why Geekish readers should care

The useful signal is that agentic AI is moving from demo language into normal developer education. Google is framing the season around three practical pressures: build faster, secure what you build, and scale on production-ready infrastructure.

That does not settle whether every team needs agents. It does show where platform companies expect the next wave of developer literacy to form.

Quick takeaways

  • DevFest 2026 is scheduled for October 1 through December 31.
  • Google says nearly a million developers and builders are expected across the season.
  • The event tracks focus on building, security, responsible AI guardrails, and scaling.
