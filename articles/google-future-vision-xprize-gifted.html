Google's Future Vision XPRIZE winner turns AI into a hopeful film pitch

The winning short, The Gifted, imagines an 11-year-old using love and code to recreate his late mother's voice and presence, then watching that creation care for a wider community.

MC
Updated September 29, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on Google's Future Vision XPRIZE announcement.

What happened

Google says independent filmmaker Jeff Synthesized won the Future Vision XPRIZE grand prize for The Gifted, a trailer made for a competition about hopeful, technology-enabled futures.

The contest was launched with XPRIZE and Range Media Partners. Google says the winner was chosen from more than 2,500 entries worldwide.

Why it feels Geekish

The premise is deeply tech-culture coded: a child rebuilds part of a loved one through voice, memory, code, and networked systems. But the pitch is not about a runaway machine. It is about whether advanced tools can be framed around empathy, grief, and care.

The prize package includes $100,000 plus $2.5 million in feature production funding. Google says its 100 ZEROS initiative is working with Range Media Partners to help bring the story to the big screen.

Quick takeaways

  • The winning project is a solo-developed trailer called The Gifted.
  • Google says the competition drew more than 2,500 entries globally.
  • The story is positioned as a hopeful AI narrative rather than another doom loop.
