Gemini 4 is reportedly close, and Google wants its AI pace back

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on The Verge's report citing Google DeepMind chief Koray Kavukcuoglu's comments to The Information.

Google's next flagship AI model may be closer than expected, and the bigger story is how loudly Google wants to signal that it is still at the frontier.

AI MODELSGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports that Google DeepMind chief Koray Kavukcuoglu told The Information that Gemini 4 is in a refinement stage and that Google is aiming to launch it much earlier than the end of the year. He also said Google plans to keep up fast-paced iteration.

Why this matters

The timing matters because The Verge notes Google has not shipped a new flagship AI model since the Gemini 3 series in November 2025, while rival frontier releases from OpenAI and Anthropic have raised pressure on Google to answer.

The missing 3.5 beat

The Verge says Google had previously talked about a major Gemini 3.5 Pro update, but that model never rolled out. Kavukcuoglu said the company stepped back to focus on faster, less powerful Flash models instead.

Geekish take

This is not just a model-number story. It is a confidence story. Google has the research depth, distribution, and infrastructure to matter at the frontier, but Gemini 4 has to show that the company can turn that machine into timely products people actually feel.

Quick takeaways

  • Gemini 4 is reportedly in refinement.
  • Google is aiming for release much earlier than year-end, according to The Verge's report.
  • The report frames the launch as a response to rival frontier-model momentum.
