Google is testing Gemini shopping with Flipkart in India

NB

Based on TechCrunch reporting linked below.

AI shopping keeps moving from "tell me what to buy" toward "help me actually buy it."

AI CHECKOUTGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports Google is testing buying from Walmart-owned Flipkart through Gemini and AI Mode in India. The test is limited to select products and users, with a broader rollout planned for later in October.

Why it matters

This is the next pressure point for AI search: not just recommending products, but sending users into a purchase flow. If the test expands, shopping answers inside AI interfaces could become a new front door for marketplaces.

Geekish take

The big question is trust. People may enjoy having AI compare options, but checkout is where accuracy, pricing, returns, and sponsored placement start to matter a lot more.

Quick takeaways

  • The test involves Gemini, AI Mode, and Flipkart in India.
  • TechCrunch says it is limited to select products and users.
  • A broader rollout is planned for later in October.
