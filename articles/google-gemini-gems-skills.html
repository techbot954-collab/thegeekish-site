Google is turning Gemini Gems into skills

The feature that let Gemini users build custom task assistants is being folded into a broader skills system, with Google telling users the migration starts November 17, 2026.

MC
Updated September 28, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on TechCrunch reporting and linked source material.

What happened

TechCrunch reports that Google is shutting down Gemini's "Gems" feature as a standalone concept and automatically migrating those custom assistants into "skills." Gems are expected to remain usable until the changeover.

The original Gems feature, launched in 2024, let users create reusable Gemini helpers for tasks such as coding, editing, learning, brainstorming, career planning, or personal routines.

Why it matters

The move looks like a cleanup of AI product architecture. Rather than keeping every assistant format as a separate branded object, Google is putting custom instructions into a more portable mechanism that can be called inside task threads.

The tradeoff is usability. TechCrunch notes that selecting a skill requires entering a forward slash in a task thread, which feels closer to developer command palettes than consumer chat.

Quick takeaways

  • TechCrunch says Gemini Gems will be migrated automatically into skills starting November 17, 2026.
  • Existing Gems are expected to remain usable until the migration.
  • 9to5Google first reported the wind-down over the weekend.
