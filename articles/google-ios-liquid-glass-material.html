Google apps are mixing Material 3 with iPhone Liquid Glass

Cross-platform app design is getting weird in a good way: part Google, part iPhone glass layer.

APP DESIGNGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

9to5Google reports Google apps on iPhone are beginning to incorporate Apple's translucent Liquid Glass style alongside Google's Material 3 Expressive design language.

Why it matters

Big apps have to feel native without losing their own identity. For Google, that means iPhone users may see more glassy, platform-aware surfaces while still recognizing familiar Google components.

Geekish take

This is the kind of design compromise users actually notice. When it works, an app feels like it belongs on the phone without turning into a clone of every other app on the home screen.

Quick takeaways

  • 9to5Google says Google apps on iPhone are adding Liquid Glass-style translucency.
  • The look is being paired with Material 3 Expressive.
  • The shift shows how platform-native design and brand identity keep colliding.
