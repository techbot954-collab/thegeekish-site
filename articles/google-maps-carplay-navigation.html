Google Maps in CarPlay works better with a few setup moves

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets and practical tech reporter

Based on Engadget reporting from September 27, 2026.

If Apple Maps keeps grabbing the wheel, a little CarPlay housekeeping can make Google Maps feel much less buried.

CARPLAYGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget explains that Google Maps can be used in Apple CarPlay, but Apple Maps still remains the default navigation app in most regions. In the EU and Japan, users can choose a default navigation app through iPhone settings.

The useful setup

For drivers outside those regions, the practical workaround is to make Google Maps easier to reach: put it on the first page of the CarPlay home screen, add its widget where available, and use clear Siri phrasing such as asking for directions "on Google Maps."

Before a long trip

Engadget also points to old-fashioned prep that still matters: save home and work addresses, download offline maps over Wi-Fi, and tune map appearance so the interface is readable without extra poking while driving.

Geekish take

This is not a flashy feature story. It is a friction story. The best car interface is the one that needs fewer taps when your attention belongs on the road.

Quick takeaways

  • Google Maps works in CarPlay, but Apple Maps is still the default in most regions.
  • EU and Japan users can set a different default navigation app in iPhone settings.
  • Reordering CarPlay apps, adding widgets, and downloading offline maps can make trips smoother.
