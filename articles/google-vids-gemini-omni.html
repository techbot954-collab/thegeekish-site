Google Vids puts Gemini Omni video tools in the browser

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Google's Workspace announcement for Google Vids and Gemini Omni.

Google is turning Vids into a wider creator tool: the company says anyone with a Google account can make videos for free, while Gemini Omni powers higher-quality browser-based creation.

GOOGLE VIDSGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Google says Google Vids is opening up so anyone with a Google account can create videos for free. The headline upgrade is Gemini Omni support, which Google describes as bringing HD video production into the browser.

Why creators should care

Browser video tools are becoming less like lightweight editors and more like full workflow assistants. If Vids can help with clips, layouts, and production polish without a traditional editor setup, it moves AI video closer to everyday creator work.

The bigger signal

Google is placing generative video inside Workspace, not just a separate lab demo. That matters because it puts AI video creation next to docs, decks, and business workflows where teams already make training, marketing, and internal videos.

Geekish take

The interesting part is not just that AI can make video. It is that video creation is being folded into normal productivity software, where the output can go from idea to shareable asset without leaving the browser.

Quick takeaways

  • Google says anyone with a Google account can create videos in Google Vids.
  • Gemini Omni is being positioned as the engine for browser-based HD video production.
  • The update makes AI video feel more like a built-in creator workflow than a standalone experiment.
