GoPro's Mission 1 Pro ILS brings swappable lenses to action cams

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on WIRED reporting and GoPro product context linked below.

The action-camera company is nudging into cinema-camera territory, and WIRED's early review says the first attempt is imperfect but genuinely fun to shoot with.

CREATOR GEARGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

WIRED reviewed the GoPro Mission 1 Pro ILS and frames it as GoPro's first serious push into interchangeable-lens cameras, while still keeping the action-cam personality that made the brand recognizable.

The creator angle

Swappable lenses matter because they let a tiny camera move beyond the one-look, ultra-wide action shot. For creators, that means more control over framing without hauling a full mirrorless rig into every setup.

The caveat

WIRED's review does not call it perfect. The interesting part is that the camera still seems to land emotionally: it is described as fun to shoot with, which is often what decides whether creator gear actually leaves the bag.

Geekish take

GoPro needed a bigger swing than another familiar action-cam refresh. The Mission 1 Pro ILS sounds like a bet that compact cameras can borrow just enough from cinema rigs without becoming expensive desk ornaments.

Quick takeaways

  • WIRED calls the Mission 1 Pro ILS GoPro's first move into interchangeable-lens cameras.
  • The review says the camera is not perfect, but is fun to shoot with.
  • The bigger story is creator gear getting more modular without losing portability.
