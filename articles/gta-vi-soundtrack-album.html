GTA VI is turning its soundtrack into a launch-day album

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Rockstar's announcement and same-day coverage from The Verge.

Rockstar is making GTA VI's music rollout feel like a proper entertainment event, with a standalone album arriving the same day as the game.

GAME SOUNDTRACKSGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Rockstar announced Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, an official soundtrack album created with Atlantic Records and set to launch November 19 alongside Grand Theft Auto VI.

What is included

The Verge reports that the album will feature 34 brand-new original tracks. The first singles are already available, and Rockstar's announcement says the album is available to pre-save on major music streaming services.

Why this matters

GTA has always treated radio, licensed music, and fictional pop culture as part of the game world. A launch-day album makes that culture portable before players even start stealing cars or finding stations.

Geekish take

This is marketing, yes, but it is smart marketing. Rockstar is not just selling a game date. It is selling the sound of a game world weeks before players disappear into it.

Quick takeaways

  • Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album launches November 19.
  • The album is being made with Atlantic Records.
  • The Verge reports the soundtrack includes 34 new original tracks.
