Honor's Magic 9 Pro Max puts cinema-camera energy on a phone

The China launch leans on Honor's new Arri collaboration, a big circular camera island, and an 8,800mAh battery.

NB
Updated September 28, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on The Verge and Honor's Arri collaboration announcement.

What happened

Honor launched its Magic 9 flagship phones in China, with the Magic 9 Pro Max carrying the loudest gadget-geek hook: a redesigned camera island shaped around Arri's classic three-lens turret language.

The Verge reports that Arri's influence is not just cosmetic. The phone supports Arri LogC3 capture, includes 14 preset Arri Look LUTs, and can use the APV 10-bit 4:2:2 codec for video work.

Why it matters

Phone camera launches usually blur into megapixel math. This one is more interesting because Honor is borrowing from cinema workflow and camera history while still chasing flagship-phone basics.

The Magic 9 Pro Max includes a 200-megapixel main camera, a 200-megapixel 3.5x telephoto, a 50-megapixel ultrawide, and what Honor says is a huge 8,800mAh battery. The phone launched first in China, while Honor has confirmed an international launch is coming without naming timing.

Quick takeaways

  • The camera island references Arri's three-lens turret design from the Arriflex 35 era.
  • Honor says the larger camera layout helps make room for the 8,800mAh battery.
  • The Verge notes the phone uses Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6.
