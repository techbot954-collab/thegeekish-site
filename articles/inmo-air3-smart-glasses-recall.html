INMO's Air3 smart glasses were recalled over overheating temples

By Jax PixelApps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on The Verge reporting and the CPSC recall notice linked below.

Smart glasses are trying to become everyday gear. This recall is a reminder that face-worn computers need boring, rock-solid safety.

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports INMO International Technology recalled its Air3 smart glasses because the left temple can overheat during extended use, creating a burn hazard.

What triggered it

According to The Verge's summary of the CPSC notice, the recall covers more than 1,600 glasses sold in the US and Canada between December 2025 and August 2026. The CPSC said there had been 10 reports of users feeling a burning sensation on the face and left side of the ear.

The fix

The Verge says INMO is offering a free software update. That makes this a very modern hardware story: a safety issue on a wearable computer being handled through code, not just a box-in-the-mail replacement.

Geekish take

Smart glasses sit on skin, near eyes, ears, hair, and batteries. If the category wants mainstream trust, thermal design and recall transparency have to matter as much as cameras, AI, and display specs.

Quick takeaways

  • INMO recalled Air3 smart glasses over an overheating left-temple burn hazard.
  • The Verge reports more than 1,600 units are covered in the US and Canada.
  • The company is directing owners to a free software update.
