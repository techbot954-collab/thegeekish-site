iOS 27 lets you tame the tiny iMessage audio button

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on Engadget's September 22 iOS 27 guide.

One of Messages' most accidental-tap-prone controls is getting easier to manage: Engadget says iOS 27 lets users hide or replace the audio button in iMessages.

SMALL BUTTON, BIG RELIEFGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports that iOS 27 includes controls for hiding or replacing the audio button in iMessages. The change targets a familiar annoyance: the record button sharing important composer space with the normal send flow.

Why people care

Messages is one of the most-used iPhone apps, so tiny interface choices get amplified fast. A button that is useful for voice notes can still be irritating if it sits where people are trying to send text.

Geekish take

This is not a blockbuster iOS feature, and that is exactly why it matters. The best platform updates often remove a small daily friction point you stopped noticing because you had already learned to work around it.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says iOS 27 can hide or replace the iMessage audio button.
  • The tweak is aimed at accidental voice-message friction.
  • It is a quality-of-life update, not a new app or paid feature.
