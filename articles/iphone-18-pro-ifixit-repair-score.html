iFixit's iPhone 18 Pro teardown gives repair a cautious win

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on WIRED's report and iFixit's teardown notes.

The iPhone 18 Pro is not suddenly a repair dream, but iFixit's teardown says Apple kept enough modular wins to earn a provisional 7/10 score.

REPAIR SCOREGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

WIRED reports that iFixit gave the iPhone 18 Pro a provisional repairability score of 7 out of 10. That matches the iPhone 17 Pro's rating and reflects repair-friendly pieces like removable camera assemblies and a battery tray held down by screws.

The catch is the way in

iFixit's teardown says the phone still has to be opened through the glued-down screen for common work like battery replacement. The team says the white plastic display frame cracked or pulled away in three of four openings, so a battery job could become a screen-risk job if repairers are not careful.

The camera is clever, not simple

The new 48-megapixel main camera includes Apple's first variable aperture in an iPhone. iFixit praised the engineering but said the tiny blades, gummy adhesives, and tight tolerances make the aperture mechanism close to non-serviceable at a component level. The practical win is that the larger camera module is still relatively easy to remove.

Geekish take

This is the repair story in miniature: modular parts are improving, but the first step still decides whether a repair feels accessible. A 7/10 score is respectable for a modern sealed flagship, yet the screen-entry risk is exactly the kind of detail that matters more than a headline number.

Quick takeaways

  • iFixit gave the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max a provisional 7/10 repairability score.
  • Camera and battery assemblies earned credit for being removable.
  • Screen removal remains the risky bottleneck, with iFixit reporting display-frame damage during its teardown.
