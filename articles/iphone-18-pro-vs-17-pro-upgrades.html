iPhone 18 Pro looks more like an S-year upgrade

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, PCs

Based on Engadget comparison coverage and Apple spec pages linked below.

The upgrade pitch is not a full redesign. It is a pile of practical Pro tweaks: variable aperture, faster silicon, longer battery life, and a smaller cutout.

APPLEGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Engadget describes the iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max as strong but incremental upgrades over the iPhone 17 Pro line, closer to an S-year refinement than a major redesign.

The camera story

The biggest hardware change is the 48-megapixel main camera gaining a variable aperture with f/1.48, f/1.8, f/2.8, and f/4.0 options. Engadget says Apple also added more manual controls and Apple Reference Image for sensor-signed RAW comparison data.

Chip and battery

Apple says the A20 Pro chip uses a 2-nanometer process, adds more memory bandwidth, and improves graphics and AI processing. Engadget notes the Pro Max gets the strongest battery-life case, while both new Pro models can reach 50 percent charge in about 15 minutes with the right adapter.

Geekish take

This is the kind of upgrade that makes sense for creators who care about camera control, battery, and sustained performance. For iPhone 17 Pro owners, the story sounds more like patience than panic.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget frames the iPhone 18 Pro line as an incremental Pro upgrade, not a redesign.
  • The main camera adds variable aperture and more manual controls.
  • Apple says A20 Pro improves bandwidth, graphics, and AI processing over the prior chip.
