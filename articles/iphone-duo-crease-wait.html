The iPhone Duo crease story needs time, not hype

A foldable phone can look pristine in a launch demo. The real question is what the hinge and display look like after real life gets involved.

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports the iPhone Duo has an impressively minimal crease at the start, but says we will need time before knowing how visible it becomes with use.

Why it matters

Creases are one of the obvious tradeoffs on foldable phones. A subtle launch-day crease is good news, but long-term wear, lighting angles, dust, pressure, and repeated folding are what turn engineering promises into everyday reality.

Geekish take

This is exactly the kind of feature reviewers should revisit later. The first impression tells us Apple can make the fold look polished; the ownership story tells us whether it stays that way.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says the iPhone Duo crease starts out impressively minimal.
  • The outlet cautions that long-term visibility will take time to judge.
  • Foldable durability is still a months-later story, not just a launch-day spec.
