The short version

Polygon recommended five detective shows for viewers who love Lanterns, noting that the series works especially well when it leans into its murder-mystery structure and central duo chemistry.

Why it matters

Superhero TV does not have to live on powers and cameos alone. When the hook is a strong case, a slow burn, and investigators who actually bounce off each other, it can pull from noir and prestige-TV habits too.

Geekish take

The watchlist framing is the signal: Lanterns is being talked about less like homework for comic continuity and more like a gateway into mystery shows.
