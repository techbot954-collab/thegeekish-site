Cleaning a laptop fan starts outside the case

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets and practical tech reporter

Based on Engadget reporting linked below.

A dusty fan can make a laptop louder and hotter, but the first move is not always opening the machine.

MAINTENANCEGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget says laptop fan cleaning should start with the vents before you reach for a screwdriver.

Why that order matters

Opening a laptop can turn a simple maintenance chore into a parts-and-warranty problem. Starting externally keeps the job simpler and reduces the chance of making a small issue worse.

Geekish take

The best repair energy is boring and patient. Clean what you can reach, check whether the laptop improves, and only escalate when the easy path fails.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget's guide says to start with the vents.
  • Reaching for a screwdriver too early can add unnecessary risk.
  • The practical goal is better airflow without turning cleaning into repair work.
