Laptop power banks are about watts, not just capacity

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PC reporter

Based on Engadget reporting linked below.

A giant battery number is not enough. Your laptop cares about how quickly that power can arrive.

LAPTOP GEARGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports that laptop power-bank shopping should focus on how fast the battery can deliver power, not only on its total capacity.

What the numbers mean

Capacity tells you roughly how much energy is stored. Wattage and USB-C Power Delivery support tell you whether the bank can keep up with a laptop while it is running.

The practical check

Look at the wattage on your laptop charger, then compare it with the power bank output rating. A low-output bank may charge slowly, fail to charge under load, or only work when the laptop is asleep.

Geekish take

This is the classic accessory trap: the biggest printed number on the box is not always the number that decides whether the setup works.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says laptop charging depends on output speed as well as capacity.
  • USB-C Power Delivery and wattage are the key specs to check.
  • Match the bank output against your laptop charger before relying on it for travel.
