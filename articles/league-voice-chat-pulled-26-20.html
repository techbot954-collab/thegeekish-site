Riot is pulling League voice chat before patch 26.20 lands

JP
By Jax PixelApps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Engadget's September 22 report.

League of Legends voice chat was close enough to headline a patch cycle, but Engadget reports Riot is removing it from version 26.20 for now.

GAME CHAT PAUSEGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports that Riot is pulling voice chat from League of Legends 26.20. The story frames the move as a late pause for a feature players were almost able to try in that update.

Why it matters

Voice chat in a competitive online game is never just a microphone toggle. It changes coordination, moderation, player safety, and the social temperature of matches, especially in a game with League's scale and history.

Geekish take

The pause is probably frustrating for players who want faster team calls. Still, a delayed communication feature is better than one that ships before the trust, reporting, and abuse controls feel ready.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says voice chat is being pulled from League of Legends 26.20.
  • The feature would have changed how teams coordinate in live matches.
  • Riot has not turned this into a final no; it is a pause for now.
