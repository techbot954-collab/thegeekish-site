Levoit's new air purifier is built for pet-smell apartments

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and hardware reporter

Based on TechCrunch reporting linked below.

The latest smart-home pitch is less sci-fi and more brutally practical: make the room smell less like the thing living in it.

SMART HOMEGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports Levoit has a new $189.99 air purifier focused on pet odors. The company says it can remove up to 70 percent of pet odor in one hour.

Why it matters

Air purifiers usually sell around allergens, dust, smoke, and broad wellness language. A pet-odor-first product is a more specific pitch for renters and small-space households.

What to check

The useful questions are practical: replacement filter cost, noise, room size, sensor quality, and whether odor removal holds up beyond controlled tests.

Geekish take

This is the kind of home gadget that wins or loses by being boringly effective. If it quietly solves a real apartment problem, it does not need much futurism.

Quick takeaways

  • TechCrunch reports Levoit announced a pet-odor-focused air purifier.
  • The device is priced at $189.99 in the TechCrunch report.
  • Levoit claims up to 70 percent pet-odor removal in one hour.
