Logitech's X3 Superstrike turns mouse clicks into tuned haptics

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, PCs, and practical tech reporter

Based on The Verge and Engadget coverage of Logitech's September 23 Pro-line announcements.

The newest pro mouse is not chasing a wild new shape. It is pushing the strange idea that a click can be tuned like a setting.

GAMING GEARGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports Logitech's G Pro X3 Superstrike launches October 6 for $199.99 in pink or black. Like the X2, it uses haptic actuators under the main buttons instead of traditional mechanical switches.

Why it matters

The update is incremental but nerdy: wireless 8,000Hz polling, up to 135 hours of battery life, a Hero 2 sensor rated up to 48K DPI, and lower weight than the previous model in the US. Engadget also notes Logitech announced several new Pro-line gaming accessories around the same window.

The Geekish angle

The click customization is still the standout. Being able to make a click subtle, stronger, or effectively silent sounds minor until you think about how much competitive gear is about repeatable feel.

Geekish take

This is premium esports hardware for people who can tell when a switch feels 5 percent wrong. Everyone else can safely admire from a distance.

Quick takeaways

  • The G Pro X3 Superstrike is priced at $199.99.
  • It keeps haptic main buttons instead of mechanical switches.
  • The new model adds wireless 8,000Hz polling and up to 135 hours of battery life.
