McDonald's drive-thru ad test turns the menu board into media space

NB
By Nova ByteAI, infrastructure, and platform strategy

Based on The Verge coverage of Bloomberg reporting.

The drive-thru screen is already a digital surface. McDonald's is testing whether the moment after you order can also become an ad slot.

RETAIL TECHGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

The Verge, citing Bloomberg, reports that McDonald's is running a limited pilot showing ads from other brands on digital drive-thru menu boards after a customer places an order.

The business logic

According to The Verge's summary of Bloomberg's report, the test is happening at company-owned restaurants. A McDonald's spokesperson told Bloomberg the company is exploring post-purchase content that customers may find helpful, relevant, or interesting.

Geekish take

This is the retail version of every screen becoming ad inventory. The useful version could surface relevant offers while people wait. The annoying version is obvious: the last quiet seconds before fries become another tiny billboard.

Quick takeaways

  • The ad pilot appears after customers place drive-thru orders.
  • The Verge says the trial uses digital menu boards at company-owned restaurants.
  • The test could create another ad revenue stream if customers tolerate it.
