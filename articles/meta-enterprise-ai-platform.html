Meta is packaging its AI stack for enterprise work

Meta announced Meta Enterprise Platform and hired MongoDB CEO Chirantan "CJ" Desai to lead the new initiative.

NB
Updated September 28, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on TechCrunch, Meta, and MongoDB source material.

What happened

TechCrunch reports that Meta is launching Meta Enterprise Platform, a new business AI initiative meant to bring Meta's AI stack to companies and developers.

The company hired Chirantan "CJ" Desai, MongoDB's CEO, to lead the effort. MongoDB said Dev Ittycheria, who previously served as chief executive, will return as interim CEO while its board searches for a permanent replacement.

The stack play

Meta says the platform will focus on turning its AI models, agents, and business tools into products that companies can deploy inside their own operations. TechCrunch says the pitch includes Muse, Meta Business Agent, Muse API, Muse Code, and other developer-facing pieces.

That makes the move less about one chatbot and more about selling the connective tissue around AI: agents, APIs, customer workflows, and business automation.

Quick takeaways

  • Meta is creating an enterprise AI initiative after launching its Muse assistant earlier in September.
  • CJ Desai is leaving MongoDB to lead Meta Enterprise Platform.
  • MongoDB said Dev Ittycheria will serve as interim CEO during the transition.
