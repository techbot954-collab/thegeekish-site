Microsoft refreshes its smaller Surface machines with Snapdragon X2 Plus

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on The Verge, Engadget, and Microsoft source material.

The compact Surface line is getting a chip bump, but the story is also about Microsoft nudging its smallest premium Windows machines into a higher-price lane.

WINDOWS HARDWAREGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports Microsoft is refreshing the 12-inch Surface Pro and 13-inch Surface Laptop with Qualcomm's Snapdragon X2 Plus chips. Engadget also notes the smaller models are getting a needed chip upgrade.

The price shift

The Verge says the new 12-inch Surface Pro starts at $1,149.99 and the 13-inch Surface Laptop starts at $1,199.99, above last year's launch prices. One practical reason: Microsoft is no longer leading with base 8GB models for these machines.

Why this matters

Surface has become a showcase for the Windows-on-Arm push. A faster Qualcomm chip keeps that pitch moving, but the higher entry prices make these machines feel less like compact defaults and more like intentionally premium portable PCs.

Geekish take

This is the tradeoff to watch in AI-era laptops: better silicon and more memory are useful, but the affordable tier keeps disappearing. If Windows-on-Arm is going mainstream, the next interesting move is not just speed. It is whether prices come back down.

Quick takeaways

  • Microsoft refreshed the 12-inch Surface Pro and 13-inch Surface Laptop.
  • Both move to Qualcomm Snapdragon X2 Plus chips.
  • The smaller Surface machines now start above $1,100, according to The Verge.
