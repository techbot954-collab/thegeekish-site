Minecraft is getting a new dimension called The Sift

JP
By Jax Pixelgaming, apps, and internet culture reporter

Based on Engadget reporting by Jackson Chen.

The new dimension is slated for the base game in 2027, after showing up first in Minecraft Dungeons 2.

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports Minecraft is getting a new dimension called The Sift in 2027. The Sift will reportedly arrive first with Minecraft Dungeons 2 later this month before coming to the base game.

Why it matters

New Minecraft dimensions are a big deal because they do more than add a biome. They can reshape exploration, progression, loot, building ideas, and the way creators package new challenges for audiences.

What to watch

The important detail is how The Sift connects to Minecraft Dungeons 2 before the main game. If Mojang uses the spinoff as a preview lane, fans may get an early read on the dimension's enemies, materials, and tone.

Geekish take

Minecraft updates hit hardest when they feel like toys and toolkits at the same time. A whole dimension gives players something to explore on day one and something to remix for years.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says The Sift is a new Minecraft dimension planned for 2027.
  • It is expected to appear first with Minecraft Dungeons 2 later this month.
  • The real test is whether it adds new systems, not just new scenery.
