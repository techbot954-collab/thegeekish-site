Morphotonics' EUR40M raise connects smart glasses to data center optics

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on TechCrunch reporting and IDC context linked below.

The same optics manufacturing story behind better AR glasses is also drifting toward data centers, where moving information with light is becoming a serious infrastructure bet.

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports that Dutch deeptech company Morphotonics raised EUR40 million to scale production of nanoimprint lithography technology used for displays and optics.

Why smart glasses care

Morphotonics makes equipment and processes for manufacturing waveguides, the thin glass or plastic parts that guide light into the wearer's eye in smart glasses and AR displays.

The data center angle

TechCrunch says Morphotonics is also pushing into co-packaged optics for data centers, where photonic integrated circuits use light instead of electricity to move data between servers and networking gear.

Geekish take

This is why "display tech" is not a tiny gadget niche anymore. The race for better waveguides and the race for faster, cooler data movement are starting to share manufacturing DNA.

Quick takeaways

  • Morphotonics raised EUR40 million, according to TechCrunch.
  • The company uses nanoimprint lithography to make AR/display optics at scale.
  • Its next target includes optical components for data centers.
