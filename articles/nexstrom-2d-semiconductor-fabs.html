Nexstrom wants 2D semiconductors to leave the lab

The chip story is not only about bigger AI clusters. TechCrunch reports that Singapore-based Nexstrom raised funding for equipment that could help manufacture 2D semiconductor materials at scale.

The short version

TechCrunch reports that Nexstrom, a Singapore startup, has raised new funding to develop equipment for chipmakers working with 2D semiconductor materials.

Why it matters

2D materials are interesting because they promise extremely thin semiconductor structures. The hard part is moving from lab-scale demos to repeatable manufacturing, which is why fab equipment is the story here.

Geekish take

This is the kind of chip news that sounds quiet until it suddenly is not. If new materials are going to matter, someone has to build the boring, precise machines that make them usable at scale.

Quick takeaways

  • TechCrunch says Nexstrom raised funding for 2D semiconductor manufacturing equipment.
  • The startup is based in Singapore.
  • The practical challenge is scaling 2D materials for chip fabs.
