Nintendo won a $4.5M Switch piracy judgment against a Reddit mod

JP
By Jax PixelApps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on The Verge reporting, Aftermath reporting, and the court judgment linked below.

Nintendo's anti-piracy playbook just produced another expensive warning for console modding communities.

GAMING LAWGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports Nintendo won a $4.5 million default judgment against James Williams, a Reddit moderator accused of distributing pirated Nintendo Switch games.

What the order says

The Verge, citing the judgment, says a Washington federal judge also ordered Williams to shut down online stores allegedly used to host libraries of pirated Switch games.

The community angle

Nintendo sued Williams in 2024. The Verge reports the complaint accused him, also known online as "archbox," of being a leading moderator in the r/SwitchPirates community and soliciting donations tied to copied games.

Geekish take

This is not just a damages number. It is a signal to emulator, modding, and piracy-adjacent communities that Nintendo is still willing to make individual operators the example.

Quick takeaways

  • Nintendo won a $4.5 million default judgment in the Switch piracy lawsuit.
  • The judgment also orders shutdowns of alleged online stores for pirated games.
  • The case targets an individual tied to Reddit's Switch piracy scene.
