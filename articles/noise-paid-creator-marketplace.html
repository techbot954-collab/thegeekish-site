Noise wants the next creator economy to start with normal people

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on TechCrunch reporting and Noise's public site.

The influencer economy usually rewards people who already have attention. Noise is pitching a messier, more scalable version: train regular users, match them with brands, and pay per view.

CREATOR MARKETPLACEGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports that Noise raised a $5.5 million seed round, bringing its total funding to $7.2 million. The platform lets everyday people join brand campaigns, create posts for TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube, and get paid per view instead of charging a traditional influencer fee.

How it works

Brands upload campaign briefs and budgets. Noise handles creator matching, training, campaign operations, and payments. The company says it has 1.5 million creators and that top creators can earn more than six figures a year, according to TechCrunch.

The creator catch

This model could lower the barrier for people who want to earn online, but it also makes creators part of a performance-marketing machine. The key details are pay transparency, creator control, and whether beginners can learn without becoming cheap ad inventory.

Geekish take

Noise is not just selling influencer marketing. It is selling a factory for small creators. That could be powerful if it gives people their first internet income, and rough if brands capture most of the upside.

Quick takeaways

  • Noise raised $5.5 million in seed funding.
  • The platform pays everyday creators per view for brand content.
  • The big question is whether this democratizes earning or commoditizes creators.
