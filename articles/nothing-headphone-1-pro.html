Nothing's Headphone 1 Pro aims at studio ears

The new $399 flagship over-ear headphones push Nothing's transparent design into a more serious audio lane.

NB
Updated September 29, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on reporting from The Verge and WIRED.

What happened

Nothing has introduced the Headphone 1 Pro, a $399 flagship pair of over-ear headphones. The Verge reports the headphones include active noise cancellation, multiple sound profiles, physical controls, Auracast support, and a triple-driver design.

WIRED says the headphones are tuned with Metropolis Studios and are being pitched as a more studio-minded step up from Nothing's less expensive audio gear.

Why Geekish readers should care

The interesting bit is not just another premium headphone launch. Nothing is trying to turn its recognizable transparent hardware style into a higher-end audio identity, while also pulling newer Bluetooth features like Auracast into a mainstream-looking product.

If the studio tuning and triple-driver setup land well, Nothing gets a cleaner argument for moving beyond design-first gadgets into enthusiast audio territory.

Quick takeaways

  • The Headphone 1 Pro is reported at $399.
  • The feature list includes ANC, Auracast, physical controls, and triple drivers.
  • Nothing is positioning the model as a flagship step above its cheaper headphones and earbuds.
