NVIDIA helped put thousands of viral protein structures in the open

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on NVIDIA's open protein dataset announcement.

The dataset adds predicted 3D protein-complex structures for more than 2,800 viruses to the AlphaFold Database.

OPEN SCIENCEGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

NVIDIA says it joined a research coalition including Google DeepMind and EMBL-EBI to release predicted 3D structures for protein complexes from more than 2,800 viruses. The data is being made available through the AlphaFold Database.

What is inside

NVIDIA says the structures were inferred with AlphaFold2 and optimization from NVIDIA BioNeMo Inference Runtime. The company is also releasing the BioNeMo Structure Prediction Pipeline used to generate the dataset.

Why it matters

Protein complexes are important because many proteins work in groups, and those shapes can matter for diagnostics, vaccines, and treatments. NVIDIA says about 30 percent of the protein interactions being added are new to science.

Geekish take

This is one of the better uses of AI hype: less "the model will cure everything" and more "here is a shared dataset researchers can test, challenge, and build on."

Quick takeaways

  • The release covers protein-complex predictions for more than 2,800 viruses.
  • NVIDIA says the work used AlphaFold2 and BioNeMo acceleration.
  • The dataset is intended to help pandemic preparedness and open science research.
