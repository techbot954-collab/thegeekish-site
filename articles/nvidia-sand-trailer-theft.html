Thieves thought they stole Nvidia hardware. They got sand

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on WIRED reporting linked below.

Sometimes the AI hardware gold rush turns into a slapstick supply-chain story.

AI SUPPLY CHAINGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

WIRED reports thieves stole two trailers from autonomous-trucking developer PlusAI near Newark, California. The trailers carried PlusAI and Nvidia logos, but they were not full of chips. They were test trailers loaded with sand.

What was actually inside

PlusAI told WIRED the trailers used simulated loads for research and development testing. Police recovered the trailers with 40,000 pounds of sand intact, and the company's truck cabs were not taken.

The bigger backdrop

WIRED connects the incident to a wider rise in theft targeting high-value tech cargo, including data-center equipment, Tesla batteries, bitcoin mining machines, metals, and enterprise networking gear.

Geekish take

The funny part is the sand. The serious part is that AI hardware has become valuable enough that even the branding on a trailer can look like a payday.

Quick takeaways

  • WIRED says thieves stole two PlusAI trailers with Nvidia branding.
  • The trailers were recovered with 40,000 pounds of sand intact.
  • The incident sits inside a broader wave of tech cargo theft.
