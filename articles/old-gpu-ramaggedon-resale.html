Your old GPU might be worth more in the RAMaggedon

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PC reporter

Based on Engadget reporting linked below.

That dusty graphics card in a drawer may not be junk if memory prices keep warping the PC parts market.

PC HARDWAREGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports that old GPUs could be worth more than expected as the current memory crunch, nicknamed RAMaggedon, reshapes parts pricing.

What changed

Graphics cards are not just processors. They also carry video memory, and memory-market pressure can make cards with useful VRAM more interesting to buyers, builders, and resellers.

What to check

The useful move is boring but practical: identify the exact GPU model, VRAM amount, condition, and recent sold prices before tossing it, trading it, or leaving it in a drawer.

Geekish take

The PC upgrade cycle has a weird second life. Sometimes the part you replaced becomes valuable again because the supply chain changed around it.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says old GPUs may be more valuable during the RAMaggedon memory crunch.
  • VRAM amount and recent sold prices matter more than vague nostalgia.
  • The smart move is to price-check old cards before recycling or selling too cheaply.
