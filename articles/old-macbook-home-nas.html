Your old MacBook can become a home NAS

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets and practical tech reporter

Based on Engadget reporting from September 27, 2026.

That aging laptop in a drawer may still have one extremely useful job left: becoming a tiny local file server for the house.

HOME SERVERGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget published a guide for turning an older MacBook into a home NAS server, giving spare hardware a local-storage job instead of sending it straight to resale or recycling.

Why it matters

A home NAS can centralize backups, media, documents, and shared files. An old laptop is not the cleanest server box, but it already has a screen, keyboard, battery, Wi-Fi, and enough compute for lightweight home use.

The Geekish read

The underrated part of old tech is that it does not need to stay useful at its original job. A retired laptop can be a low-drama home server, especially for people who want to learn before buying dedicated NAS hardware.

Quick takeaways

  • An older MacBook can still work as a small file-sharing server.
  • A home NAS is useful for backups, local media, and shared household storage.
  • The setup is best treated as a practical reuse project, not a perfect enterprise server.
