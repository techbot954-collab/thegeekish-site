Old-school credit card scams are still alive

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on WIRED Kernel Panic reporting linked below.

AI scams are loud, but physical cards, mailers, QR codes, and magnetic stripes still have teeth.

SECURITYGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

WIRED reports that old-school payment scams are far from dead. Recent examples include fake replacement cards sent through the mail, QR-code banking lures, and magnetic-stripe skimmers.

The mailbox trick

WIRED says waves of physical-card scams have appeared in countries including Portugal, France, and Germany. The letters may claim a card is expiring and direct victims to a QR code or URL that leads to a fake banking page.

The swipe problem

The report also points to magnetic-stripe skimming, including attacks on EBT cards. WIRED notes the FBI says EBT card skimming has grown more popular with scammers since around 2021.

Geekish take

The scam stack is not replacing old tricks with new ones. It is layering them. Treat mailed cards, QR codes, payment terminals, and urgent banking messages with the same suspicion you already give sketchy texts.

Quick takeaways

  • WIRED says fake mailed replacement cards can steer victims to fake banking sites.
  • Magnetic-stripe skimming still matters, especially where swipe-only cards remain common.
  • Avoid swiping when possible and inspect terminals that look altered or broken.
