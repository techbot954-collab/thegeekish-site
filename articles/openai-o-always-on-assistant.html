OpenAI's reported always-on assistant raises the email-agent stakes

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on BleepingComputer reporting from September 27, 2026.

The assistant is not announced, so the important part is not the branding. It is the direction: AI tools are moving from chat windows toward persistent task handlers.

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

BleepingComputer reports OpenAI is testing a new always-on assistant called o, and says references to the unannounced feature briefly appeared on OpenAI's website.

Why it matters

Email is where agent promises get real fast. A persistent assistant that can monitor, draft, organize, or act around inbox context would need strong consent, logging, permissions, and mistake recovery.

The Geekish read

Treat this as a signal, not a launch. The useful question is what kind of guardrails an always-on assistant needs before people trust it with one of their messiest and most sensitive workflows.

Quick takeaways

  • The reported feature is unannounced, so details may change.
  • Always-on assistants raise bigger permission questions than normal chatbots.
  • Email handling is powerful because it mixes private context with external action.
