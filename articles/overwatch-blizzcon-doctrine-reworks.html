Overwatch's BlizzCon reveal puts Sombra in support and Doctrine on deck

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Blizzard's BlizzCon reveal post and Xbox Wire's developer interview.

BlizzCon turned into an Overwatch roadmap moment: a vampire-inspired support hero, a new Icelandic prison escort map, and two reworks that could change team comps fast.

HERO SHOOTER WATCHGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Blizzard says Doctrine is Overwatch's Hero 54, a vampire-inspired Support Hero tied to Doomfist and the Reign of Talon story. The character had a limited-time Hero Trial around BlizzCon and is slated for a full Season 5 debut.

The map is part of the story

The reveal also includes Watchpoint: Grimsvotn, an Escort map set at a remote Icelandic volcano prison. Blizzard frames it as the place where Doomfist reconnects with Doctrine, which makes the map more than a backdrop for payload chaos.

Sombra is changing lanes

The wildest gameplay note is Sombra. Blizzard says Season 5 brings major Sombra and Roadhog reworks, including Sombra moving from Damage to Support. On the Official Xbox Podcast, Overwatch developers described a version of Sombra that stays near teammates more often while keeping stealth and hacking as part of her identity.

Geekish take

This is the kind of hero-shooter update that matters even if you only check in between seasons. A new support hero is one thing; moving a long-running disruption character into the support role is the spicy part. If Blizzard lands that rework, Season 5 could feel like more than a content drop.

Quick takeaways

  • Doctrine is Blizzard's next Overwatch Support Hero and is planned for Season 5.
  • Watchpoint: Grimsvotn is a new Escort map set at an Icelandic volcano prison.
  • Sombra is moving from Damage to Support as part of a Season 5 rework, while Roadhog is also getting reworked.
