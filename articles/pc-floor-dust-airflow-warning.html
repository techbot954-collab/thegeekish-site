Your PC probably deserves better than the floor

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets and practical tech reporter

Based on Engadget reporting from September 27, 2026.

Putting a tower under the desk feels harmless until the machine starts breathing carpet dust and living in the kick zone.

PC FLOOR WARNINGGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget says a desktop PC is usually better off the floor, or at least protected from the floor-level problems that come with dust, poor airflow, and everyday accidents.

What to watch

The issue is not that a floor instantly kills a computer. It is that floor placement can make intake vents work harder, invite more dust, and put ports and panels closer to feet, pets, spills, and vacuum cleaners.

The Geekish read

This is wonderfully boring advice because it is cheap: lift the tower, keep the vents clear, and clean around it. That can do more for long-term reliability than buying a cooler you did not need.

Quick takeaways

  • Floor placement can expose desktop PCs to more dust and everyday physical risks.
  • Airflow matters more than the exact aesthetic of the setup.
  • A simple riser or desk-side stand can be a practical upgrade.
