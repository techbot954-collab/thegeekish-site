Peak XV is making its Surge seed checks bigger

TechCrunch reports Peak XV raised the investment ceiling for its Surge seed-stage platform to $5 million per company while unveiling an 18-startup cohort.

MC
Updated September 29, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on TechCrunch reporting.

What happened

Peak XV Partners has announced Surge 12, a new cohort of 18 companies, and TechCrunch reports the program now has a higher investment ceiling of up to $5 million per startup.

The firm invested more than $50 million across the cohort, which Peak XV says has collectively raised more than $90 million in seed funding.

Why it matters

The larger ceiling is a signal about seed rounds getting heavier, especially when startups are building in capital-hungry areas like AI, robotics, space, healthcare, and deeptech.

TechCrunch also notes the cohort is more global than a simple India-only read would suggest: more than half of the companies are based in India, while 13 of the 18 are targeting global markets.

Quick takeaways

  • Surge's investment ceiling is now up to $5 million per company, up from $3 million.
  • Surge 12 includes 18 startups spanning AI, robotics, space, consumer products, healthcare, music, and fintech.
  • Peak XV says Surge has backed more than 180 startups since launching in 2019.
