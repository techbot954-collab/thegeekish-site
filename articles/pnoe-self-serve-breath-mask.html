The short version

TechCrunch reports PNOE is launching a sleeker self-serve version of its breath-analyzing mask on October 1. The company says the system lets gym-goers measure VO2 max and other metabolic markers in eight minutes without a trained operator.

Why it matters

VO2 max used to feel like a lab test, not a kiosk-style fitness feature. If self-serve metabolic testing works well, gyms and coaches could offer more personalized training feedback without scheduling a specialized technician.

Geekish take

This is useful only if the experience stays simple and the numbers are explained clearly. Fitness tech is already drowning users in dashboards; the win is turning breath data into decisions people can actually use.

Quick takeaways

  • TechCrunch says PNOE's new mask launches October 1.
  • The self-serve test is designed to take eight minutes.
  • The system targets VO2 max and other metabolic markers.
