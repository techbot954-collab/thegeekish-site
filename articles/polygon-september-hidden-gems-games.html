The short version

Polygon published a September hidden-gems list for players who got buried under the month’s bigger releases.

What crowded the calendar

Polygon points to a nonstop month that included Marvel’s Wolverine, Onimusha: Way of the Sword, Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave, Control Resonant, and more.

Why it matters

Release pileups can flatten attention. Smaller games often do not need to be worse to get ignored; they just need to launch next to louder names with bigger marketing machines.

Geekish take

This is the exact kind of list worth saving. The best game you play in a crowded month is often the one that did not dominate the trailer cycle.

Quick takeaways

  • Polygon says September was one of the busiest recent months for new games.
  • The outlet’s list spotlights three games that may have been missed between larger releases.
  • The broader lesson: release timing can bury good games even when the games themselves are strong.
