Rabbit's OS3 turns the R1 comeback into an app story

JP
By Jax PixelApps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on WIRED's report on Rabbit OS3 and Rabbit's early-access page.

Rabbit is back with OS3, but the headline is not another orange gadget. It is an AI agent that lives across the screens people already use.

AI AGENTSGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

WIRED reports that Rabbit is launching OS3, a cross-platform "agentic operating system" that can run through a desktop browser, Telegram, iMessage, and as an update for the Rabbit R1.

What changed

The company once argued that dedicated AI hardware could sidestep apps. OS3 is a pivot toward software: WIRED says it connects to desktop software, files, and web pages through a local agent node for Windows, Mac, or Linux.

The OS3 pitch

Rabbit CEO Jesse Lyu told WIRED that one account can link up to five machines, with OS3 deciding which device should perform a given task. The system uses a bring-your-own-key model for AI providers such as OpenAI or Anthropic, instead of a monthly Rabbit subscription.

Geekish take

Rabbit's most interesting move is admitting the phone and computer were already the right surfaces. The new bet is whether an agent can stitch those surfaces together well enough to make the old R1 idea feel practical.

Quick takeaways

  • OS3 is available through a browser, Telegram, iMessage, and the R1.
  • Rabbit says users bring their own model API keys or local models.
  • WIRED reports Rabbit is no longer manufacturing the R1 and has no plans for an R2.
