Netflix's Ray Gunn trailer brings Brad Bird back to retro sci-fi

By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Ars Technica's trailer report and Netflix's video.

Brad Bird's long-gestating animated detective story finally has a trailer, and the vibe is film noir with jet packs, aliens, and laser guns.

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Ars Technica reports Netflix released the trailer for Ray Gunn, the new animated feature from Brad Bird, director of The Iron Giant, The Incredibles, and Ratatouille. The movie is slated for December.

Why geeks are watching

The premise is pure retro-future bait: a hard-boiled detective story with sci-fi comedy, flying cars, laser guns, aliens, and noir style. Ars notes Bird has been carrying the idea for decades, with a script co-written by Matthew Robbins.

What makes it fun

The title itself is a pun, but the appeal is bigger than wordplay. Bird's best movies mix clean visual storytelling with kinetic action, and Ray Gunn looks like a chance to see that skill aimed at old-school sci-fi pulp.

Geekish take

Streaming animation can feel algorithmic, but a weird, long-simmering Brad Bird sci-fi noir is exactly the kind of specific project that cuts through the queue fog. Put this one on the December watch list.

Quick takeaways

  • Netflix released the trailer for Brad Bird's animated sci-fi noir Ray Gunn.
  • Ars says the movie is coming to Netflix in December.
  • The story blends detective noir, retro-futurism, comedy, and action.
