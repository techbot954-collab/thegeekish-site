Razer's Kiyo V2 Pro webcam aims 4K60 at serious streamers

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PC hardware reporter

Based on Engadget's same-day Razer Kiyo V2 Pro report linked below.

Razer's new pro webcam is not chasing bargain-bin video calls. It is aiming at creators who want high frame rates without stepping into camera-rig territory.

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports Razer's Kiyo V2 Pro webcam can capture 4K video at 60 frames per second and will cost $300. That puts it in the premium creator-webcam lane, not the casual meeting-camera pile.

Why 4K60 matters

Higher resolution gives creators more room to crop, while 60 fps keeps motion cleaner for hand-heavy demos, desk setups, and stream overlays. The useful question is whether the sensor, autofocus, software, and lighting performance match the headline spec.

The price check

At $300, the Kiyo V2 Pro has to compete with strong webcams and entry-level mirrorless camera setups. The pitch works best for creators who want a simpler USB setup without giving up crisp motion.

Geekish take

Webcams have finally stopped being an afterthought for creators. If Razer nails image processing and reliability, 4K60 could be a meaningful upgrade for streamers who do not want a whole camera cage on the desk.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says the Kiyo V2 Pro records 4K video at 60 fps.
  • The webcam is expected to cost $300.
  • The real test will be image quality, autofocus, and low-light performance.
