Razer's Tartarus Pro refresh keeps the weird gaming keypad alive

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on Engadget's report and Razer's product page for the Tartarus Pro.

The one-handed gaming keypad is still a very specific gadget. Razer's refresh is a reminder that niche PC gear survives when it gives power users controls they cannot get from a normal keyboard.

GAMING GEARGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports that Razer refreshed the one-handed Tartarus Pro keyboard with improved switches. Razer's product page describes the Tartarus Pro as a PC gaming keypad with Razer Analog Optical Switches and 32 programmable keys.

Why this matters

Gaming keypads are not mainstream accessories, but they are sticky for MMO players, macro-heavy PC gamers, and anyone who wants a dedicated control surface next to the mouse.

The control pitch

Razer says the analog optical switches can register different input levels depending on how far a key is pressed. Its example: a half press can walk, while a full press can run, similar to an analog stick.

Geekish take

This is wonderfully specific hardware. Not everyone needs a one-handed keypad, but the people who do tend to really know why, and that is exactly why the category refuses to disappear.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says Razer refreshed the Tartarus Pro with improved switches.
  • Razer lists 32 programmable keys on the Tartarus Pro product page.
  • The analog optical switch pitch is about more nuanced control.
