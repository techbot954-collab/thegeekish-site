Revolut wants UK shoppers to pay with a face scan

JP
By Jax PixelApps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Engadget's report and Revolut's Pay with Smile privacy notice link cited there.

The checkout pitch is speed. The obvious question is whether shoppers want biometric payments at the coffee counter.

PAYMENT TECHGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports Revolut is piloting a facial-recognition payment system called Pay with Smile in the UK. The trial starts at select Kiss the Hippo coffee locations in London and is tied to Revolut Register, the company's new hospitality point-of-sale hardware.

How it works

According to Engadget, customers enroll first. At checkout, the system compares biometric authentication data against selfies registered during sign-up. Revolut says the product is designed to reduce payment friction and has no processing fees for merchants.

The privacy pitch

Engadget says Revolut claims personal data stays inside its own application environment, facial embeddings used for a transaction are deleted after the sale is confirmed, and merchants do not keep them in-store.

Geekish take

This is exactly the kind of convenient tech that needs a bright privacy spotlight. A faster coffee checkout is easy to understand. A face-based payment habit is a bigger cultural shift, especially when trust in facial recognition is still fragile.

Quick takeaways

  • Revolut is testing Pay with Smile at select London coffee shops.
  • The system uses enrolled selfies and biometric authentication data to verify payment identity.
  • Revolut says transaction embeddings are deleted after checkout, according to Engadget.
