The short version

Engadget reports Roland released a Minions-themed keyboard complete with playable gibberish sounds. The outlet filed it under entertainment news and summarized it bluntly: "That's pretty weird." Fair.

Why it matters

The story sits in the wonderfully odd overlap between music gear, licensed pop culture, and novelty hardware. It is not a spec-war gadget; it is a reminder that tech companies also ship things designed to make people laugh, gift, collect, or post screenshots.

The Geekish angle
