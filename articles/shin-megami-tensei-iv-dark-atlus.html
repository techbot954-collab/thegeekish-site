Shin Megami Tensei IV still shows Atlus had a darker lane

JP
By Jax PixelGeek culture and science watcher

Based on Polygon reporting from September 27, 2026.

Persona made Atlus feel approachable. Shin Megami Tensei IV kept the studio's darker, stranger RPG muscle alive.

GAMESGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Polygon looks back at Shin Megami Tensei IV, the Nintendo 3DS RPG released 13 years ago, as proof that Atlus could still lean into darker, harder-edged role-playing after Persona 3 helped broaden the company's audience.

Why it matters

That matters because studio identities can flatten over time. When one franchise becomes the mainstream face, older or weirder design instincts can look like side quests instead of core DNA. SMT IV is the reminder that Atlus had both lanes.

The Geekish read

The fun part of this retrospective is not nostalgia for difficulty by itself. It is the contrast: social-link comfort on one side, apocalyptic demon politics on the other, both living under the same publisher roof.

Quick takeaways

  • Polygon frames SMT IV as a darker Atlus counterpoint to Persona's broader popularity.
  • The story is a retrospective, not a new game announcement.
  • The appeal is about studio identity, tone, and why old handheld RPGs still shape fan expectations.
