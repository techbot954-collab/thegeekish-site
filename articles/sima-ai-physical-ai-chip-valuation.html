SiMa.ai's new valuation puts edge AI chips back in the physical world

The edge-computing startup says it has reached a $1.45 billion valuation while pitching its stack at robots, cars, drones, and other machines that cannot wait on the cloud.

MC
Updated September 28, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on SiMa.ai and TechCrunch reporting.

What happened

SiMa.ai says it has reached a $1.45 billion valuation with $500 million in total funding as it scales its physical AI platform for humanoids, automotive systems, drones, and industrial machines.

TechCrunch reports the company raised a $150 million Series C led by Fidelity and Amplify. The pitch is straightforward: more AI is moving into devices that need low-latency decisions near sensors instead of round trips to a data center.

Why it matters

AI infrastructure usually gets framed as giant cloud clusters. Edge AI asks a different question: what happens when the model has to run inside a robot, a car, a camera, or a drone with tight power, heat, and latency limits?

That makes SiMa.ai's funding a useful signal. The next AI hardware fight is not only about bigger accelerators. It is also about whether smaller systems can make fast decisions in messy physical environments.

Quick takeaways

  • SiMa.ai says it has reached a $1.45 billion valuation and $500 million in total funding.
  • TechCrunch reports the newest round is a $150 million Series C led by Fidelity and Amplify.
  • The company's focus is physical AI workloads in machines such as humanoids, cars, and drones.
