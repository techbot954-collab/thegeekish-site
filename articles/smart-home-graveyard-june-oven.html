The smart home graveyard just got another warning sign

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on The Verge reporting linked below.

A smart oven can still be an oven after the cloud goes dark. But that is not the same as keeping the product people actually paid for.

SMART HOMEGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports that Weber shut off June Oven's app and cloud services after buying the company in 2021 and stopping oven production in 2022. June ovens can still cook, but app control, updates, new AI food recognition, and support are gone.

The bigger pattern

The Verge places June alongside a growing list of connected products that have lost core smart features, including Nest Secure, early Nest Thermostats, Belkin WeMo devices, Neato robot vacuums, Brava smart ovens, Logitech Pop buttons, Bose SoundTouch speakers, and Sengled bulbs.

What survives

Some products degrade instead of dying outright, and local APIs, Zigbee, Thread, HomeKit, or Home Assistant integrations can help. But The Verge notes June is not getting an open-source lifeline, with Weber citing June's IP inside Weber Connect.

Geekish take

A connected gadget should not become a subscription to a company's future attention span. The smart-home winners will be the products that keep useful local control when their clouds age out.

Quick takeaways

  • The Verge says June Oven's app and cloud services went dark this week.
  • The oven still works as an oven, but key smart features and support are gone.
  • The story adds pressure for local control and longer software commitments in smart-home gear.
