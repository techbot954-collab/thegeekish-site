Snap wants Specs Intelligence to follow you from iPhone to AR glasses

JP
By Jax PixelApps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on The Verge's report on Specs Intelligence and Snap's broader Specs rollout.

Snap is previewing an AI assistant that can connect to accounts, remember personal context, and eventually surface information inside its new Specs AR glasses.

WEARABLE AIGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Snap is introducing Specs Intelligence, an AI assistant that The Verge says is available in preview on iOS now, with a waitlist for a fuller early-access Mac experience and a planned role on Snap's Specs AR glasses.

What it is supposed to do

According to The Verge, Snap pitches the service as "anticipatory" AI: it can connect chosen apps and accounts, build context around goals, routines, relationships, and priorities, then surface useful reminders before a meeting or trip.

The glasses angle

The interesting bit is not just another chatbot. On Specs, Snap says personal context could be placed directly into view when relevant, turning AR glasses into a lightweight heads-up layer for briefings, travel plans, and work context.

Geekish take

This is the same trust test every personal AI assistant keeps running into: the more useful it gets, the more access it asks for. Snap says personal content from connected accounts will not train or fine-tune its AI models or serve personalized ads, but users still have to decide whether an assistant that knows their day is helpful or too intimate.

Quick takeaways

  • Specs Intelligence is previewing on iOS and is planned for Mac and Specs.
  • Snap describes it as an assistant that can anticipate useful actions.
  • The product depends on users trusting Snap with connected-account context.
