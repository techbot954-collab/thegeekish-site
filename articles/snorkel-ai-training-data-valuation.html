Snorkel AI's $3.5B valuation shows training data is still the gold rush

NB
By Nova ByteAI, chips, and infrastructure reporter

Based on TechCrunch reporting on Snorkel AI's Series E.

The AI model race still needs a less flashy ingredient: high-end data. Snorkel AI just raised like that layer is not slowing down.

AI STARTUPSGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports that Snorkel AI raised a $350 million Series E at a $3.5 billion valuation, nearly tripling the $1.3 billion valuation from its previous Series D round 17 months earlier.

What Snorkel sells now

TechCrunch says Snorkel moved from data labeling automation into completed datasets and reinforcement-learning environments. The company describes this as data-as-a-service, mixing software, models, synthetic data, and subject matter experts.

Geekish take

Everyone talks about bigger models, but the scramble for better data is just as important. Snorkel's raise is a reminder that the companies supplying training fuel can become major AI infrastructure players without looking like consumer AI apps.

Quick takeaways

  • TechCrunch says the round was led by Insight Partners and S32.
  • Existing investors including Addition, Lightspeed, Greylock, GV, and Wells Fargo participated.
  • Snorkel says its annualized revenue run-rate is $375 million, according to TechCrunch.
