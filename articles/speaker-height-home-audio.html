Speaker height can make your home audio less clear

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets and practical tech reporter

Based on Engadget reporting linked below.

Before buying new gear, check the room. Speaker placement can make voices feel oddly detached.

ROOM SETUPGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget says too much speaker height can make dialogue and fine details sound less direct in a home sound system.

Why placement matters

Home audio is not only about bigger speakers or a louder receiver. If the sound is coming from the wrong vertical position, voices and subtle effects can feel less connected to the screen.

Geekish take

This is the kind of low-cost tech fix people skip because it is not shiny. Move the setup first, shop later.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says excessive speaker height can reduce directness.
  • Dialogue and fine details are the parts most likely to suffer.
  • Room setup can matter as much as the gear itself.
