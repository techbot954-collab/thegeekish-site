Spotify Running Mode wants your playlist to keep pace

JP
By Jax PixelApps, games, and culture reporter

Based on Engadget reporting from September 27, 2026.

Workout music has always been a personal algorithm. Spotify is trying to make that algorithm more literal while you run.

AI WORKOUT AUDIOGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget's guide says Spotify Running Mode is available on iOS and Android and is built around AI-powered running features meant to help users match music to their stride.

The useful part

Running playlists can break a workout when the tempo feels wrong. A mode that adapts around pace could make music feel less like background noise and more like a training tool, especially for runners who use rhythm to stay consistent.

Geekish take

The feature lands in the sweet spot for consumer AI: it does not need to sound profound, it just needs to remove a tiny bit of friction. If your playlist stops fighting your legs, that is enough.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says Spotify Running Mode works on iOS and Android.
  • The feature uses AI-powered running tools to help match music to movement.
  • The appeal is practical: fewer playlist breaks during a run.
