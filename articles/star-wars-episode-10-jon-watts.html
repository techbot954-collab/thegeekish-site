Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Polygon reports Jon Watts has come aboard to direct Simon Kinberg's upcoming Star Wars movie, which Polygon says is expected to be Star Wars: Episode 10.

Why fans will care

Watts is best known for Marvel's Spider-Man trilogy, which makes this a familiar franchise bet: a director who has already handled giant IP expectations, returning characters, and fan-pressure storytelling.

What is still unclear

The actual story, cast, timeline, and how directly this continues the Skywalker Saga remain the big unknowns. For now, the director attachment is the meaningful signal.

Geekish take

Star Wars keeps promising fresh starts, then circling back to legacy gravity. Watts could bring a cleaner adventure rhythm, but Episode 10 will need more than a safe pair of hands to feel necessary.

Quick takeaways

  • Polygon reports Jon Watts is attached to direct the upcoming Simon Kinberg Star Wars movie.
  • The film is expected to be Star Wars: Episode 10.
  • Story, cast, and release details are still the pieces to watch.
