Tuck brings email-style snoozing to messy browser tabs

Tabs are tiny open loops. Tuck treats them like messages you can dismiss until the right moment.

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

WIRED reports Tuck is a free browser add-on that lets users snooze open tabs until later, bringing an email-style hide-and-return workflow to browser clutter.

Why it clicks

Most tab managers ask users to sort everything now. Snoozing is gentler: it removes the tab from sight while preserving the promise that it can return when it is actually useful.

Who it helps

The idea fits researchers, students, writers, shoppers, and anyone whose browser turns into a pile of half-finished thoughts by lunchtime.

Geekish take

The best productivity tools often steal good patterns from somewhere else. Email snooze made inboxes less panicky; browser snooze could do the same for tab overload.

Quick takeaways

  • WIRED reports Tuck is a free browser extension for snoozing tabs.
  • The tool hides tabs temporarily instead of forcing immediate cleanup.
  • It borrows one of email's more useful habits for browser overload.
