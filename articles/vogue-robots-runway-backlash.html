Vogue's robot runway moment became the story

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on TechCrunch reporting and Vogue World context.

A fashion show about craftsmanship ended with humanoid robots, and the most viral reaction was basically: wait, why are the robots here?

ROBOT RUNWAYGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports Vogue World Milano closed a segment called The Age of Intelligence with humanoid robots from Unitree walking into the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II to Gigi D'Agostino's "L'Amour Toujours."

Why it went viral

The Milan show was marketed around Italian craftsmanship and "the human touch" in an age of technology, which made the robot finale feel extra strange to viewers online. TechCrunch notes that reaction clips and jokes about the robots overtook discussion of the actual fashion.

The tech angle

The backlash lands because robots, AI, and luxury branding are all carrying a lot of baggage right now: labor anxiety, surveillance concerns, energy questions, and the feeling that tech elites are being invited into every cultural room.

Geekish take

This is a useful reminder for brands: "add robots" is not a neutral creative choice anymore. If the technology reads like a stunt, it can swallow the whole show.

Quick takeaways

  • TechCrunch says Vogue World Milano featured Unitree humanoid robots on the runway.
  • The moment clashed with the event's human-craftsmanship theme.
  • Online reaction focused more on the robots than the fashion.
