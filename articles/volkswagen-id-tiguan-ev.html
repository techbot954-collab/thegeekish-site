Volkswagen is turning the ID.4 into a nameplate lesson

The ID.Tiguan replaces VW's retired ID.4 crossover, which says a lot about how mainstream EVs are being sold now: less mystery badge, more familiar car name.

JP
Updated September 28, 2026

Concise Geekish brief based on Volkswagen and The Verge reporting.

What happened

Volkswagen announced that its recently retired ID.4 crossover will be replaced by the upcoming ID.Tiguan. The Verge reports the new electric Tiguan is expected in Europe in early 2027, with a U.S. arrival planned for a later date.

The Verge also notes that VW says the ID.4 sold 950,000 units since 2021 and ranked among Volkswagen AG's most successful battery-electric model series. Production at VW's Chattanooga factory ended in April 2026.

The geeky bit

The naming shift is the story. VW's early EV push leaned hard into the ID brand, but the Tiguan name already means "compact SUV" to a lot of buyers. That familiarity may matter more as EVs move from early adopters into ordinary family-car shopping.

The Verge says VW is also bringing back more physical controls, including window, volume, and climate controls, after years of touchscreen-heavy interiors. That is a small detail with big "people yelled and the buttons returned" energy.

Quick takeaways

  • The ID.Tiguan is replacing the retired ID.4 crossover.
  • The Verge reports Europe gets it in early 2027, with the U.S. planned later.
  • VW is leaning on recognizable nameplates as EVs become more mainstream.
