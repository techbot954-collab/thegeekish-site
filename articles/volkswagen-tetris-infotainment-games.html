Volkswagen is putting Tetris in the dashboard

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Engadget's September 23 report.

The car-screen app race has officially reached the "yes, your dashboard can play Tetris" stage.

DASHBOARD GAMESGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports Volkswagen is bringing AirConsole games to 2027 vehicles, with Tetris, Pac-Man Championship Edition, and Uno Car Party among the named titles. The system uses the vehicle's infotainment display and phones as wireless controllers.

Where it is showing up

The AirConsole platform is planned for 2027 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, Tiguan, Golf GTI, Golf R, and ID vehicles. Engadget says the launch library includes 20 games, and certain vehicles can sync ambient lighting to gameplay.

The subscription part

Access requires Volkswagen's In-Vehicle Premium service. Engadget reports most 2027 vehicles include three complimentary months, after which the service costs $149 per year. The game library is only available while parked.

Geekish take

This is equal parts charming and very 2026: classic games, subscription car software, and phones as controllers. The important part is the parked-only limit. Tetris in the dashboard is fun. Tetris at speed is a cursed idea.

Quick takeaways

  • Volkswagen's AirConsole setup is coming to select 2027 vehicles.
  • The library starts with 20 games, including Tetris and Pac-Man Championship Edition.
  • Games require In-Vehicle Premium and can only be played while parked.
