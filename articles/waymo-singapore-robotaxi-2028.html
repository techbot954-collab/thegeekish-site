Waymo is aiming for Singapore robotaxi rides in 2028

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on Waymo's announcement, The Verge reporting, and Singapore LTA guidance.

Singapore is becoming Waymo's next international proving ground, but the path still runs through mapping, supervised testing, and local safety assessments.

GLOBAL ROBOTAXISGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

Waymo says it is coming to Singapore, and The Verge reports the company is working toward public fully autonomous ride-hailing there in 2028.

The rollout path

The Verge reports Waymo vehicles will begin arriving in Singapore in the coming months, followed by mapping and autonomous testing with human safety drivers in 2027.

The local hurdle

Singapore's Land Transport Authority says autonomous vehicles must pass CETRAN safety assessments before deployment on public roads. That means the announcement is not the same as instant robotaxi rides.

Geekish take

This is Waymo choosing a dense, transit-savvy city as a serious test of whether robotaxis can travel well across rules, roads, and cultures. The interesting part is not just the car; it is the city accepting the car.

Quick takeaways

  • Waymo announced Singapore as its next international expansion market.
  • The Verge reports public fully autonomous rides are targeted for 2028.
  • Singapore requires AV safety assessment before public-road deployment.
