Waymo is opening robotaxi rides to teens in Nashville

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets, creator gear, and transportation tech reporter

Based on TechCrunch's report on Waymo's Nashville teen-account launch.

Waymo is turning teen riders into part of its expansion plan, starting with parent-linked accounts in Nashville.

ROBOTAXISGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

TechCrunch reports that Waymo will allow teenagers as young as 13 to use its robotaxi service in Nashville through accounts connected to a parent or guardian.

How it works

Once activated, teen riders can hail autonomous rides within Waymo's Nashville service area. TechCrunch says teen account holders can bring up to three guests ages 13 to 17, though Waymo did not share how those ages would be verified.

Why Nashville matters

Nashville is Waymo's second teen-account market after Phoenix. The company launched paid service in a 50-square-mile Nashville area in April 2026, and its robotaxis recently became available through Lyft there.

Geekish take

This is not just a new rider setting. It is Waymo testing whether autonomous rides can become a default family logistics tool, especially for trips where parents want an option other than a teen driver.

Quick takeaways

  • Waymo teen accounts are expanding to Nashville.
  • The accounts must be connected to a parent or guardian.
  • Waymo told TechCrunch more teen launches are planned where rules and operations allow.
