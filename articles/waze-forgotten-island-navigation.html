Waze turns your commute into a Forgotten Island side quest

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on Waze's September 17 announcement.

The newest Waze movie tie-in adds custom voice navigation, a themed mood, and a Jeepney-inspired car icon for DreamWorks Animation's Forgotten Island.

MAP APP ENERGYGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Waze says it is launching a global English-language Forgotten Island experience ahead of the DreamWorks Animation film's September 25 release. The setup puts Jo, voiced by H.E.R., in the passenger-seat role for turn-by-turn directions.

What changes in the app

Drivers can activate Jo's custom voice navigation, switch on a "Nostalgic" Mood represented by Jo's retro paint-splattered boom box, and replace the normal navigation arrow with "Jo's Jeepney," a bright hero ride that Waze says nods to Filipino cultural heritage.

Why it fits Geekish

Navigation apps have quietly become tiny entertainment platforms. A movie partnership inside Waze is not just an ad unit; it is a temporary skin for one of the most boring, repeated interfaces in daily life.

Geekish take

The clever part is that Waze is not asking users to watch a trailer. It is putting the character into a task people already do, which is the difference between a normal promo and a little piece of app culture.

Quick takeaways

  • The Forgotten Island Waze experience is available globally in English.
  • It includes H.E.R.'s Jo voice navigation, a Nostalgic Mood, and Jo's Jeepney vehicle icon.
  • The film is scheduled to arrive in theaters September 25, 2026.
