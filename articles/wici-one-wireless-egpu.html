WiCi One wants to make the external GPU wireless

MC
By Mika Circuitgadgets, creator gear, and hardware reporter

Based on Engadget reporting linked below.

The pitch is simple: more gaming horsepower, fewer cables, and a price that keeps it firmly in enthusiast territory.

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

Engadget reports the WiCi One is an external GPU that uses Wi-Fi to turn devices into gaming rigs, with preorders starting at $1,999.

Why it clicks

External GPUs have always promised laptop-flexibility with desktop-class graphics. Making that link wireless pushes the idea into a cleaner, more living-room-friendly shape.

The catch

The price alone makes this a niche buy for now. The real story is whether wireless graphics can feel reliable enough for gamers who notice every hitch.

Geekish take

This is the kind of product that starts as expensive future bait. If the experience works, cheaper versions could make old laptops, handhelds, and tablets feel far less disposable.

Quick takeaways

  • Engadget says WiCi One uses Wi-Fi as a wireless external GPU link.
  • Preorders start at $1,999.
  • The big question is whether cable-free GPU power can feel responsive enough for real play.
