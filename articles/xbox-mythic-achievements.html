Xbox Mythic achievements sound like Microsoft's Platinum trophy moment

JP
By Jax Pixelapps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on The Verge's September 23 report about Xbox PC app and dashboard references.

Achievement hunters may finally get the Xbox equivalent of a single shiny prize for fully clearing a game.

GAMINGGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports that a new Xbox PC app update includes references to Mythic achievements, which would appear when a player completes all achievements in a game. The feature also reportedly has its own unlock sound.

Why it matters

The report says players may be able to track Mythic achievements through the Xbox dashboard and display their Mythic count on profiles. Some Xbox Insider testers have already seen references in early dashboard builds.

The Geekish angle

That puts Xbox closer to PlayStation's Platinum trophy language, but with Microsoft's own achievement branding. It is a small feature with outsized emotional value for completionists, backlog trackers, and people who treat games like checklists with boss music.

Geekish take

This is pure gaming culture infrastructure: one badge, one sound, one more reason to chase the last annoying collectible.

Quick takeaways

  • The feature has appeared in Xbox PC app and early dashboard references.
  • Mythic achievements are tied to completing all achievements in a game.
  • The Verge reports profile counts and a unique unlock sound are part of the references.
