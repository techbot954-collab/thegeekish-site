Xbox's studio reset puts Activision on the next Halo

JP
By Jax PixelApps, social tech, and internet culture reporter

Based on The Verge reporting.

Microsoft's Xbox reorganization is moving major franchises into new homes, including a future Halo title led by Activision.

XBOX RESETGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the sources linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports Microsoft is making another major Xbox studio shake-up. Activision is set to develop the next Halo title through a new purpose-built team, while a smaller Halo Studios team will keep supporting current Halo games.

The bigger shuffle

The report says Microsoft is also cutting 268 roles across Halo Studios, first-party studios, and Xbox management layers. It also describes Rare and World's Edge moving under Activision, Obsidian moving under Bethesda for a new Fallout project, and Playground Games and Turn 10 merging around Forza and Fable.

Why gamers care

Halo has been tied to Microsoft's console identity for decades, so moving the next flagship installment to Activision is not just org-chart housekeeping. It signals Xbox is willing to rebuild its most familiar franchises around fewer, larger publishing groups.

Geekish take

This is the kind of corporate reset that players feel later: fewer studios, bigger bets, and a lot of pressure on the next Halo to prove the shuffle was worth the pain.

Quick takeaways

  • The Verge says Activision will lead development of the next Halo title.
  • Microsoft is cutting 268 additional Xbox roles in the reported reset.
  • Forza, Fable, Fallout, Sea of Thieves, and Age of Empires are also part of the reorganization.
