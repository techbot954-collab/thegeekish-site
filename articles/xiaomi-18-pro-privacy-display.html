Xiaomi's 18 Pro phones make privacy screens feel less clunky

MC
By Mika CircuitGadgets, creator gear, and PCs reporter

Based on The Verge's September 23 reporting.

The privacy-screen idea is usually a compromise. Xiaomi is trying to make it feel like a normal flagship feature instead.

PHONE HARDWAREGeekish sourced quick read

Original Geekish context based on the source linked below.

The short version

The Verge reports Xiaomi launched the 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max in China with a more flexible take on privacy-display tech, rear screens like last year's 17 Pro models, two 200-megapixel cameras, and Qualcomm's new Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chips.

Why it matters

Privacy displays usually narrow the viewing angle so strangers nearby cannot easily read the screen. The catch is that they can make the phone worse for everyday sharing, media, and quick glances. Xiaomi's pitch is that the feature can be more adjustable instead of always-on awkward.

What to watch

The Verge says a global launch is confirmed for later this year, which matters because last year's 17 Pro models skipped international markets. If Xiaomi ships the hardware widely, privacy screens could move from niche laptop behavior into mainstream phones.

Geekish take

This is the kind of phone feature that sounds small until you use it in public every day. If Xiaomi makes it easy to toggle and tune, privacy mode could become the next quiet flagship flex.

Quick takeaways

  • Xiaomi launched the 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max in China.
  • The phones pair privacy-display tech with returning rear screens.
  • The Verge says global availability is planned for later this year.
