## Facebook post
Elementor patched a WordPress flaw with a nasty trick: one malicious link could make a logged-in admin create an attacker-controlled admin account.

BleepingComputer and Patchstack say Elementor 4.3.0 and 4.3.1 were affected. The fix is in 4.3.2.
