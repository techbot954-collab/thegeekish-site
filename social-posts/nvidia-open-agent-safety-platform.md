AI agents are getting more authority, so Nvidia is pitching a very hardware-brained answer: put them in boxes they cannot talk their way out of.

OpenShell sets runtime boundaries. Sentry watches from a separate BlueField-backed layer. Nvidia says bad agent behavior can be quarantined in milliseconds.
